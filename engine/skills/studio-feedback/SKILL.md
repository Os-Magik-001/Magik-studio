# What the studio approved and sent back

These rules come from real rounds of feedback on delivered videos (October 2026). When another skill disagrees, these win, unless the client's own script or brief says otherwise.

**The client's script comes first**
- Read the client's script and brief before planning anything. They can forbid flashes, effects on punchlines or meme stickers, fix the captions' wording, or correct a line ("Dubai, or don't buy.", not the brief's "or don't buy it").
- Captions say what the script says, with the client's own words for things ("units" where the speaker says "floors" was a client correction).

**Every word heard**
- A client sent a video back for "more time between the transitions, make sure we hear every word well pronounced": tight cuts had clipped nine words and left a "yo-" fragment. Clients want air after each line more than a short video.
- Every in and out point sits in a real gap between words, never on a word: about 0.1s before the first word and 0.2-0.35s after the last, or the middle of a shorter gap. A "t" or "k" release belongs to the word before it.
- Two shots from the same clip never overlap: an overlap repeats a syllable ("Nor-Normally", "O-One"). Never drop the middle of a line ("built in Merce-").
- Shots that continue the same take (a reframe or a punch-in) join seamlessly, with no fade.
- Speech starts right after a transition, not under it, and each line gets room to land before the next transition.
- Length follows the content: going over a minute is fine when the client says so (Instagram Reel ads stop at 60s). Never squeeze lines to hit a length.

**Cuts and transitions**
- Hard cuts are the default. A transition marks a change of place or scene, nothing else.
- Approved: a zoom-through (a radial zoom blur from a subject into the matching subject of the next shot); a push-through (push and blur, a 3-frame blend, settle); cutting on the camera's own whip pans; a door or object swinging into the lens used as a wipe; one speed ramp per video; a freeze-frame character intro.
- Rejected: 3D card-swing transitions ("the 3D transitions are shit"), 3D walk-through endings, flashes and bounces.
- Punch-ins change size by 0.3 or more (1.0 to 1.3-2.0). Smaller changes (1.0, 1.1, 1.15) read as glitches.
- A cold open can put one clip's line over another clip's picture when it makes a stronger hook (an opening clap, a reaction).

**Captions and fonts**
- One clean caption system per client, 2-5 words on screen, no full stops. Once a client approves a caption font, keep it on every later video ("keep the caption font the same").
- Turned down by the owner: Playfair 600 in cream, Montserrat 500, Inter Light.
- Approved: Satoshi 700 with Instrument Serif italic accents; and the "AD1" system: Helvetica Neue Light 300 (tracking about -0.045em) for the line, key words under it in Helvetica Neue Bold or Bold Italic or in Times New Roman Italic, punchlines in Times New Roman Bold Italic with a yellow glow (#ffe36e) lighting up letter by letter, numbers that count up.
- Titles can sit behind the speaker's head (the person cut out in front) when the shot has room above them.

**Motion design and 3D**
- The owner wants rich motion design ("make better motion designs", "more 3D motion designs"), but each piece serves the story: a glass wishlist that comes back for every ask and gets ticked off in the reveal, a stat card that counts, a gauge, check pills, a call-to-action pill.
- 3D that read as premium: chrome or black-lacquer extruded letters, gold letters dropping in one after another with a soft pop and the payoff word slamming with a camera knock, buildings with lit window facades and a warm fog. Plain dark boxes with gold bands looked cheap.
- Every graphic that appears gets a soft sound effect (this owner's preference beats the "sparse effects" rule), never on a joke or a punchline.

**Colour**
- Natural by default: "natural video grading is fine". No warm grade and no vignette unless asked.
- When a grade is asked for: first balance each shot (its whites and exposure), then one filmic look with tamed greens and a light split-tone, blacks kept black.

**Music and sound effects**
- The music must be clearly audible. A bed 12-17 LU under the voices read as "no music". The approved balance is about 9-11 LU under the voices while they talk, back up in the gaps and full on the reveal; final loudness -14 LUFS.
- Land the track's own landmarks on the edit: its drop on the reveal, its stop under the last line. Move them by looping or trimming whole bars, never mid-bar.
- Music almost off under every punchline.
- Smooth effects only: pick them by their attack and tail, never one with a cut-off tail. A hit or clap on the opening grabs attention when it stays the loudest moment of the opening.

**End card**
- A client's QR code or permit is held still with its white border, never stretched, tilted or animated, so it scans.
