# Information overlays on the word

## When to use
A person talks to camera and lists concrete things (colours, prices of a menu, steps, tools, ingredients). Measured on @tomfavre (« Le noir et le bleu, c'est joli, mais ajoute du rouge néon », 33.4 s, one take, no cut).

## What the reference does
- **One take, one framing.** He sits close and slightly off-centre, pointing up with one finger on each « mais ajoute ». No cuts, no zooms: the overlays are the edit.
- **A column of swatches, top left** (x 30–200, y 180–520): a rounded square per colour with its name in tiny tracked caps (« NOIR PROFOND », « BLEU ÉLECTRIQUE »). Each appears on its colour's word, the third one on « ajoute du… ». The column clears and refills for each example.
- **A mock-up, lower middle** (y 1150–1500, over his hand and chest, never his face): a website hero (« BUILT DIFFÉREMMENT », a car) shown in the first two colours. On the third colour it recolours: the accent spreads through the glow, the title, the car's light. That change is the payoff of each example.
- **A flat card first.** Each new example opens with a solid card of the base colour in the mock-up's place for 0.3–0.5 s, then the mock-up arrives in that palette: the viewer sees the colour before the design.
- **Four examples of 5.5 s each, then nothing.** When he moves to the conclusion (« Trois couleurs bien choisies… ») every overlay leaves and he talks alone with captions: abstract lines get no graphic.
- **Captions** in small white sentence case, 2–3 words, under his face at mid-height, no box.

## The recipe for our clients
1. Find the repeated pattern in the talk (« X et Y, mais ajoute Z », « avant / après », « étape 1, 2, 3 »). Each repetition is one example and gets the same layout, so the viewer learns it once.
2. List the nouns that can be shown (a colour, a dish, a price, a product). Each gets a small card that appears on its word, ±2 frames, in a fixed column away from the face.
3. Give each example one payoff change in one big object (the mock-up recolours, the plate fills, the number counts up).
4. Abstract lines, the conclusion and the call to action: no cards, only captions.
5. The face stays clear (text-placement-and-legibility); cards never move once placed except to leave.

## Motion
- Swatches: in over 6 f (scale 0.85 to 1, a small rise, expo-out), 3 f apart when two come together; out together over 4 f when the example ends.
- The recolour: 10–14 f, a soft wipe or a cross-dissolve through a glow, never a cut.
- Sound: a soft paper tick for each swatch at the quietest level, a short swell under the recolour, nothing else (motion-sfx-sync).
