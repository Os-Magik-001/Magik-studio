name: ad
description: Structure for paid ads that sell a product or offer
formats: ad
---

**Length**
- Default to 15, 30 or 45s, whichever the script fits. Ads are bought in those slots, so land just under one.

**Structure by length** (when the script doesn't set one)

| Length | Beats |
|---|---|
| 15s | hook 0–3s, problem 3–7s, solution or product 7–12s, call to action 12–15s |
| 30s | hook 0–3s, pain 3–8s, product in use 8–18s, proof 18–24s, call to action 24–30s |
| 45–60s | the same spine, with more product in use and a second proof |

- The opening ~5s works as a unit: the hook, a concrete number or proof, then an open question the ad answers later.
- If the ad could run as a skippable YouTube ad, or the brief says so, the brand and product must be clear within the first 5 seconds. Earn attention first, though: don't open on the brand name or a logo.

**What sells**
- **One job per ad:** one product, one offer, one call to action. Leave out footage that doesn't serve a beat.
- **Product hero:** one slow, beautiful moment of the product, a slow push or slow motion on your best close-up, held at least 1.5s.
- **Show, don't design:** real product-in-use footage and real results beat titles describing them.
- **Proof:** place one proof point (a result, a number, a reaction) right after the product's best moment, as a hard cut. Don't stack several proofs back to back.
- **Prices:** a price, when the brief gives one, stays on screen at least 1.5s.
- **Call to action:** specific, on screen at least 3s, and its title appears when the call to action is first said and stays to the end.
- **Headline takeovers:** a full-frame headline title at most 3 times, entering right after a cut, never over a face.

**Style**
- Follow the brand kit exactly: colors, fonts and tone. Pick the look from the brand, usually `natural` or `cinematic`, unless the style rules say otherwise.
- Transitions: clean and restrained. Hard cuts, and a `crossfade` or `dip_to_black` into the end card. No novelty wipes unless the brand is playful.
- Every claim, price, offer and statistic on screen must come from the script or brief. Never invent them, and never trim approved ad copy just for pacing. If the script is vague, ask in `open_questions`.
