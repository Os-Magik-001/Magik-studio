name: content-types
description: Editing defaults for common kinds of content (product, place, tutorial, story)
formats: all
---

Find the closest match to what the footage and brief are about, and start from its defaults. The script, brand kit and editor's notes override them.

**Product reveal**
- Build to the product rather than showing it whole at once: tease with detail close-ups, then the hero.
- Give the hero one slow push-in (1.0 to 1.15 or more) held 2s or more, plus macro detail inserts of 1 to 2s each.
- Look: `natural` or `punchy`, clean and true to the product's real colors.

**Place reveal** (real estate, hospitality, travel, architecture)
- Use slow pushes and pulls on wides, and let spaces breathe (3 to 5s per shot).
- Start wide to set the space, then move to the details that sell it.
- Look: `warm` for golden-hour or cozy spaces, `cool` for modern or minimal ones.
- Usually no talking footage. Use titles for the key facts (location, size, price, only if they're in the brief).

**Tutorial or demo**
- Clarity over mood. Show each step long enough to follow (about 3 to 6s), in order, with no skipped steps.
- Use static framing, or punch-ins to show detail. Avoid flashy transitions and speed changes on the steps themselves, though you can speed up waiting or repetitive parts.
- Number the steps with titles when there are three or more.
- Look: `natural`, with even exposure across shots.

**Story-driven** (testimonial, founder story, behind the scenes)
- Follow the person and let the strongest line lead.
- Hold emotional beats longer. Cut away to what they describe.
- Look: matched to the mood, usually `natural`, `warm` or `cinematic`.

**Pacing by tone**
- **Energetic social:** shots of 1 to 3s.
- **Brand film:** 3 to 5s.
- **Luxury or cinematic:** 5 to 8s, with slow moves and few cuts.
