---
name: content-types
description: Editing defaults for common kinds of content (product, place, tutorial, story, vlog, news)
formats: all
---

Find the closest match to what the footage and brief are about, and start from its defaults. The script, brand kit and editor's notes override them. For mostly visual promos, also see trailer-and-montage.

**Product reveal**
- Build to the product: tease with detail close-ups, then show the hero.
- Give the hero one slow push-in held 2s or more, plus macro detail inserts of 1–2s each.
- Look: `natural` or `punchy`, true to the product's real colors.

**Place reveal** (real estate, hospitality, travel, architecture)
- Use slow pushes and pulls on wides, and let spaces breathe (3–5s per shot).
- Order the shots wide, then medium, then detail, then a person enjoying the space.
- Look: `warm` for golden-hour or cozy spaces, `cool` for modern or minimal ones.
- Use titles for the key facts (location, size, price), only if they're in the brief.

**Tutorial or demo**
- Clarity over mood. Show each step long enough to follow (3–6s), in order, with no skipped steps.
- Use static framing, or punch-ins for legibility (see punch-ins-and-reframing), returning to wide between steps.
- Speed up waiting or repetitive parts, never the step itself.
- Number the steps with titles when there are three or more.
- Look: `natural`, with even exposure.

**Story-driven** (testimonial, founder story, behind the scenes)
- Follow the person and let the strongest line lead.
- Hold emotional beats longer. Cut away to what they describe, between their sentences.
- Look: `natural`, `warm` or `cinematic`, matched to the mood and gentle on faces.

**Vlog or day-in-the-life**
- Three acts:
  1. About 25%: a hook and what's coming.
  2. About 50%: the body, organized by place or activity.
  3. About 25%: the peak, then close on a feeling.
- Order by story or time, never by file name. Close on a shot with a person in it.
- Average shot 3–4s: under 2s in montage bursts, and as long as the speech when someone talks. Alternate long and short.
- Aim for about 60% close and medium shots, 40% wide.
- Section titles go over footage at the start of a section, about 2–3s, not on black cards.
- Mute shots with no useful sound, like wind noise or traffic on point-of-view footage.
- Travel and transit shots work at 2–3x, muted. Speech always stays at 1x.

**Factual or news**
- Open on the outcome. End on the latest confirmed fact.
- No effects. Hard cuts, and crossfades only between sections.
- Never put two shots next to each other in a way that implies one caused the other when the footage doesn't show it.
