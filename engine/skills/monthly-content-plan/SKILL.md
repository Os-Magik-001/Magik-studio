**How many and which**
- Plan exactly the number of videos the client's pack includes this month.
- Spread them over the pillars, roughly in the strategy's mix. At least one video proves (result, review, number) and at most one sells (an offer).
- Open with the strongest idea: the first video of the month should have the clearest hook and the most visual payoff.
- Put seasonal moments in the month they belong to, early enough to be shot and approved.

**Every idea**
- A title the viewer would want to watch, in the client's language.
- The pillar it serves and the goal it moves.
- The hook in one line: what we see and hear in the first two seconds.
- A brief of 3 to 5 sentences: what the video is about, who's in it, where it's filmed, what we must see, the call to action, the length (usually 15 to 45 s).
- Why it should work: the research pattern or the brand fact it uses.

**Make it easy to shoot**
- Group ideas that share a place and people, so the videographer can shoot several in one session (say so in the brief: "same morning shoot as video 2").
- Keep each video doable in about two hours of shooting with the client's own team.
- Use what the researcher found (hooks, structures, sounds) but never copy a reel.
- Never repeat a video the studio already made for this client.
