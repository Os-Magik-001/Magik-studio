---
name: calls-to-action
description: Ending a short video with one call to action that matches the client's goal
agents: scriptwriter, planner
---

- One call to action per video. Two halves both.
- Match it to the goal: a DM keyword for leads ("Écris CROISSANT en DM"), "link in bio" for online sales, a day and time for foot traffic ("Samedi, avant 10 h"), a deadline for pre-orders.
- Make it specific and easy: a word to type, a place to come, a date. "Contact us" doesn't work.
- Say it and show it: the last line spoken and the same words on screen, held at least 2 s. Repeat it in the post's caption.
- Put it in the last 3 seconds, after the payoff, never before it.
- Value videos can end softly ("Save this for Saturday") instead of selling.
