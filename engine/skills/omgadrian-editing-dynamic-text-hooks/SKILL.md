# Text, hooks and annotations (dynamic)

@omgadrian's signature is big text that amplifies one word; it is never a transcript. Use it for dynamic clients. For skits, office-comedy-reels' text limits win wherever they differ.

1. **Hook title in the first 3 s.**
   - Use `stacked` (or `hero`) in the top third, with `pop`, 2-4 short lines, the second line biggest.
   - Show it during the first spoken line (2.5-3.5 s), then remove it.
   - Write it as a headline that teases or frames, not a copy of the words. Voice: 'Most people get this wrong'. Title: 'YOU'RE LOSING THEM IN 3 SECONDS'.
   - About 75% of viewers watch on mute, so the title has to work on its own.
   - For The Boyz, the POV caption from office-comedy-reels is the hook instead.
2. **One keyword hit at a time.**
   - Build emphasis as a small lead-in line plus one giant keyword ('is what creates' / 'QUALITY'): `stacked`, keyword at hero size, `pop`.
   - Snap the in-point to the keyword's word edge and hold it for the rest of the phrase (1-2 s).
   - Turn on text behind the person when the word crosses the head and the background is plain.
   - At most one hit per 8-10 s, and never two text blocks on screen at once.
3. **One brand style.** Choose one text style and one colour per client. Reuse them for every title, keyword and label in every reel, and never switch styles mid-reel.
4. **Section markers.**
   - At 'first', 'step two' or 'number three', show a `hero` numeral or 'STEP 2' (centre or top, `pop`, about 1 s) over the first shot of the section.
   - For specs and shot types, show a `label` at the top ('24MM', 'WIDE SHOT', 'SHOT ON IPHONE') for the length of that shot.
5. **Label every comparison.** When two shots are compared, put a `label` on each: 'BEFORE' / 'AFTER' or 'WITHOUT ND' / 'WITH ND', in the same position on both.
6. **Cards for proof.**
   - When the line refers to a screenshot or clip (a comment, a profile, a settings menu, a past reel), show it as a `card` in the upper half.
   - Bring it in on the first words that mention it and hold it 2-3 s.
   - Add a `circle` on the detail being pointed at ('these numbers right here').
7. **Comic annotation, once per reel.**
   - A narrator aside starts with an asterisk ('*CONCERNED', '*TALKING ABSOLUTE GIBBERISH'): `label`, small, top or upper-middle, `pop`, placed on the reaction shot itself.
   - It is the punchline, so it lands with the reaction, never after the button.
   - A kept flub works the same way: `hero` 'NEVERMIND' on the restart, with one `pop` sound.
   - One annotation gag per reel.
8. **Corrections without recutting.** For a misnamed product or an out-of-focus shot you keep, add a `label` starting with '*' ('*RODE VIDEOMIC PRO+'). Place it next to the object or at the top, for that shot only.
9. **Captions under big text.**
   - Captions keep running in the client's preset.
   - For tutorial or creator clients without a preset: `editorial`, small, lower-middle above the bottom 20-25%, 3-5 words per screen, no highlights.
   - Keep big text out of the caption band.
10. **Endings: CTAs on non-skit reels only.**
   - If the speaker says a keyword ('comment GRID'), show `comment_cta` with that exact word, timed to the line.
   - On 'follow', show a `follow_pill`.
   - A `stacked` end card over the last shot is held at least 1.5 s after the last word.
   - In skits, no CTA graphics: end on the laugh.

## Example
Voice: 'Your camera isn't the reason your videos don't look cinematic. It's the lens.'
- 0-3 s: `stacked` at the top, 'IT'S NOT / YOUR CAMERA', `pop`, off at the end of the first sentence.
- On 'lens': `stacked` 'It's the' / 'LENS', text behind the person, 1.2 s.
- The next keyword hit comes no sooner than 8 s later.

## Example
Skit reaction: Ghost stares after the CEO's pitch.
- `label` '*STILL PROCESSING', small, at the top, `pop`.
- It comes in at the start of the stare and goes out with the cut.
- No sound effect and nothing after the button.
