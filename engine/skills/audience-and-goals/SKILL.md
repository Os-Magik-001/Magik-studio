**The audience**
- Describe real people, not demographics alone: who they are, where they live or pass by, when they scroll (morning commute, lunch, late evening), what they want to feel or know, and what stops them from buying today.
- Usually there are two audiences: the regulars to keep and the new people to win. Say which videos speak to which.
- Note the language they live in (French, English or both) and the words they use.

**Goals**
- Turn each need into a goal with a number and a deadline where you can: "fill the counter on weekend mornings", "300 holiday pre-orders by December 20", "grow Instagram from 2.1K to 8K by March", "10 bookings a week from Instagram".
- One goal per need. 3 to 6 goals in all.
- Give each goal the signal that shows it working: saves and shares for education, comments and DMs for conversation, profile visits and link taps for sales, store visits or bookings for local businesses.

**From goals to content**
- Awareness goals need reach: hooks, trends, shareable moments.
- Trust goals need proof: process, people, results, reviews.
- Sales goals need a clear offer and one call to action.
