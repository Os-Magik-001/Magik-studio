The motion templates are what make an edit feel produced rather than just cut. Use them on the moments that deserve a beat, never as wallpaper.

**Templates and when to use them**
- **`character_intro`** (2.8–3.5s, full frame): presents a person.
  - Put a `hold` shot under it: the same clip and framing as the shot before it, with `start` at the frame you freeze on and `hold` equal to the motion length.
  - Freeze on a frame where the person is sharp, facing camera, with head and shoulders in frame, ideally mid-expression (a smirk, raised eyebrows).
  - `title` is the name. `subtitle` is the role in 3–6 words.
  - `lines`: 2–3 stats. Make them funny or telling, written as `LABEL|0-100` ("VISION|100", "SLEEP|8"), or one plain line ("Never takes the stairs").
  - Use it once per person, the first time they matter. Put it right after their first line, so we hear them, then freeze.
  - Sound: a `hit` at the freeze, plus a `whoosh` 0.4s later as the card flips in.
- **`cast_lineup`** (2.8–3.8s, full frame): introduces a group, for an opener or "meet the team".
  - Give 2–6 `people`, each with the clip and time of a clean frame of them (they're cut out automatically), a `focus` on them, a name and a short role.
  - Put it over a `hold` shot or over a shot you don't need to see. Place it right after the hook.
- **`title_3d`** (1.5–3s, overlay): a big claim, a chapter title, the episode tease ("EPISODE 2" with subtitle "tomorrow"), or a punchline word.
  - Keep it to 1–4 words, at `top` or `center`, where it won't cover a face. Put a `hit` under it.
- **`stat_card`** (2–3s, overlay): one proof number, such as views, money, a time saved or a price. `title` is "from|to" ("0|1000000"), `subtitle` the label.
- **`lower_third_3d`** (2–3.5s, overlay): names a speaker while they keep talking, with no freeze. Use it when a full intro would be too much, at `top` or `center`.

**Rules**
- Any full-frame template (character_intro, cast_lineup) needs a `hold` shot of the same length under it, or the speech under it gets covered. Shift every later caption, title and sound effect by the hold's length.
- Use 2–5 motion moments in a 30–45s video. Leave at least 3s of plain footage between two of them.
- Captions skip hold shots by themselves (nothing is spoken there). Don't put a hero word or caption on top of a full-frame template.
- `accent`: the brand's accent color (red for The Boyz). Use null to use the brand kit's.
- The look must stay readable at phone size: short names, short roles, at most 3 stat lines.
