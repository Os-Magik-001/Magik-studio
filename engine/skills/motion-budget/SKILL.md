# Motion budget

**Tiers**
- **A — the big moments** (a 3D explainer, a glowing stack, a freeze-frame intro, a slam): 2–4 per 30 s, at least 5 s apart. Each lands on a named word or a number.
- **B — supporting graphics** (cards, pills, hero words, a counter): every 2–5 s for creators (measured), every 8–10 s for tutorials.
- **C — captions**: 13–20 changes per 10 s for creators (measured).

**Rhythm**
- Something changes every 1–4 s: a cut, a graphic, a move. A motion piece never holds still for more than 1.5 s, except the final logo.
- Vary the gaps between tier-A moments by at least 30%: equal spacing reads as a template.
- The biggest moment comes in the last 25% before the call to action.
- An emphasis zoom at most every 10–15 s. Transitions only where the set-up, place or section changes (transitions).

**Where motion never goes**
- On a punchline, its laugh, or the payoff shot itself: the picture is the point there.
- Comedy and series: only name cards and the POV or premise line.

**One world**
- Keep one material, one light and one background family for the whole video (one-world-explainer). Mixing styles between scenes is what makes an edit look assembled.
- When the owner or a client asks for « more motion », add a 3D explainer of the thing being named (a skin block for a treatment, a lash on a rod), not more text.
