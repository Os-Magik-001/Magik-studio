name: reel-analysis
description: Breaking down one reel: the hook, the structure, the pacing, the text, the sound and why it performed
agents: researcher
---

For each reel, from its contact sheet, transcript and numbers:

**The first two seconds**
- What we see in frame one, what is said first and when, what text is on screen. Name the hook type: question, bold claim, number, visual surprise, POV, before/after, "watch this", controversy.
- Whether the hook is visual, verbal and written at the same time (the strongest reels usually are).

**Structure and pacing**
- The beats with rough times (hook 0–2 s, setup, turn, payoff, call to action).
- Cuts per second and where the pace changes. Re-hooks in the middle ("but here's the thing…").
- Length versus how much happens: dead air or a slow middle explains weaker reels.

**Text, captions and graphics**
- Caption style (size, position, color, words per line), titles, emojis, arrows, numbers.

**Sound**
- Voice, music or trend sound; the song when known; whether the cut follows the beat.

**Why it performed**
- One honest sentence. Separate the content (the idea) from the execution (the edit) and from luck (a famous account, a trend wave).
