---
name: podcast-clip
description: Cutting talking-head and podcast moments into a short clip
formats: podcast_clip
---

**Choose the moment from the transcript**
- Pick one self-contained idea: a strong opinion, a surprising fact, a story with a punchline, or a clear piece of advice.
- It must make sense with no context. If the script or brief names a topic, find that.
- Start on the sentence that grabs attention, even if it comes after a setup. Lift a later strong line to the front as the hook if it doesn't change the meaning.
- End on the payoff line, not on a trailing "so, yeah".

**Cutting speech**
- Never cut mid-word or mid-sentence. In and out points go in the gaps between transcript lines.
- Tighten by removing ums, false starts, long pauses and tangents.
- Hide every cut inside a continuous take with a framing change: alternate the wide framing (zoom 1.0) and a punch-in (about 1.3, within the clip's sharp-zoom limit) on each cut. That way a jump cut reads as a deliberate second angle.
- Set `focus` on the speaker's face. With two people, frame whoever is talking.
- Keep the speaker's sound on every shot. No speed changes on speech.

**Text and finish**
- Captions throughout, word-accurate to the transcript, timed to the speech. This matters more here than in any other format.
- Add a title in the first 2s that frames the topic or teases the payoff.
- Look: `natural`, with `adjust` fixes to match the cameras if the shots differ.
- Transitions: hard cuts only, except a `crossfade` or `dip_to_black` into the end card.
- Length: 30 to 60s by default.
