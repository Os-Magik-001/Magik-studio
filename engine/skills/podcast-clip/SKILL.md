---
name: podcast-clip
description: Cutting talking-head and podcast moments into a short clip
formats: podcast_clip
---

Choose the moment with highlight-selection, cut the speech with picking-takes, and frame the speakers with talking-head. This skill adds what's specific to podcasts.

**The moment**
- Choose one self-contained idea: a strong opinion, a surprising fact, a story with a punchline, or clear advice.
- If the brief names a topic, find that one.
- Length: 30 to 60s by default. Completeness beats hitting the length exactly.

**Cutting**
- Hide every cut inside a continuous take with a framing change: wide (zoom 1.0) and close (about 1.3, within the clip's sharp-zoom limit). Alternate them at phrase changes.
- With two people, frame whoever is talking. Cut to the listener only for a genuine reaction.
- Keep the speaker's sound on every shot, and no speed changes on speech.
- Leave the picture up after the last words long enough to read the final caption. Don't end the instant the audio ends.

**Text and finish**
- Captions throughout, word-accurate to what's heard, one line each. This matters more here than in any other format.
- Add a title in the first 2s that frames the topic or teases the payoff. If the clip needs context, the title gives it.
- Look: `natural`, with small `adjust` fixes so the cameras match.
- Transitions: hard cuts only, except a `crossfade` or `dip_to_black` into the end card.
