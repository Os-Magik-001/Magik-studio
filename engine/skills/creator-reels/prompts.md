# Creator-reel prompts

Each style below has two parts:

- **Film:** what to shoot, for the brief or shot list. The edit can only be as good as the footage.
- **Notes for Claude:** paste this into **Launch the edit** on the video page.

The edit engine reads `SKILL.md` in this folder whenever the notes say "creator reel" or name a recipe. These prompts aren't loaded by the engine; they're for the team.

Swap the bracketed parts for your own.

---

## 1. Talking-head creator reel (like @justyn.ai and the "Claude edits my reels" reel)

**Film**
- One person talking to camera in 4–8 different setups: desk, sofa, standing, walking in, a selfie-style close-up. Keep each setup 5–15s.
- Leave the bad takes in. The engine picks the last clean one.
- Get one walk-toward-camera or hand-to-lens moment for the hook or the payoff.
- Add 10–20s of the raw process or behind-the-scenes, if the story has one.

**Notes for Claude**
> Creator reel, recipe 1, punchy. Hook in the first 2s with the boldest claim as a top title from frame 0. Cut every pause. Change setup every 4–8s and alternate wide/close inside a setup. One hero word as a top title every 2–4s (numbers, promises, payoffs), captions 1–3 words at chest height. Show what I say when I name a change. Speed up any raw-footage moment 2–3x with a title. Keep the line "[proof line, e.g. this reel was edited by Claude]". End on "Comment [KEYWORD]" as a title held to the end, no end card. Under [45]s.

**Variation, moody:**
> …same, but after "[line that asks for moody]" grade the rest darker and desaturated, and make the hero words bigger and more frequent.

## 2. Multi-speaker countdown (like the five-realtor Miami invite)

**Film**
- 3–5 people, each in their own location, each saying one line that carries a number: "Five of us…", "Four states…", "Three hours…", "Two things…", "One night…".
- Seated or standing, static camera, warm light. 5–8s each.
- The last person says the call to action.

**Notes for Claude**
> Creator reel, recipe 2 (multi-speaker countdown). One speaker per 5–7s in countdown order. Each speaker's number is a hero title at the top the moment it's said (FIVE, FOUR, THREE, TWO, ONE), captions 1–3 words at chest height. Hard cuts between speakers with at most one slide whip. Warm soft look. Last speaker: "Comment [KEYWORD] to [benefit]" as a title held ~2s, no end card. 30–35s.

## 3. Walk-and-talk promo (like the exposurefx realtor reel)

**Film**
- One presenter on a gimbal walking toward the camera in 4–5 outdoor locations (bridge, sidewalk, field, overpass). Wide and medium-close.
- Fast, punchy script, about 60 words for 15s, opening by calling out the viewer: "[Realtor] from [city]?"

**Notes for Claude**
> Creator reel, recipe 3 (walk-and-talk). New location every 2.5–4.5s, alternate wide and medium-close, white flash into the second shot. Punchy look, blue skies. Hero title per sentence ([CITY]?, ATTENTION, [BENEFIT]), captions 1–3 words at chest height. Push in on any shot where I walk toward camera. End on "DM us / Comment [KEYWORD]" with no end card. 15s.

## 4. Process proof (like the "Netflix promo made with AI" reel)

**Film**
- One continuous phone shot of the finished work playing on a monitor, with the editing app or tool visible in front.
- Steady, 15–25s.

**Notes for Claude**
> Creator reel, recipe 4 (process proof). Keep the take whole, trim only the ends. One top caption for the whole video: "[reaction line, e.g. Claude ate with this one]". No other text, no end card. Natural look.

## 5. Animated SaaS ad (like the 16:9 "skill file" ad)

That reel has no filmed footage. Every frame is animated in code (3D cards, UI mock-ups, kinetic type) under a voiceover. **This engine edits filmed footage, so it can't produce that ad yet.** The closest it can do:

**Film**
- Screen recordings of the product in use.
- One presenter shot for the hook and the call to action.

**Notes for Claude**
> Ad, 16:9 if the brief says so. Hook "What if [bold promise]?" as a title from frame 0, then the product in use with a hero title per benefit ("Just describe it", "Ships in minutes"), proof line "[every frame came from one file]", call to action "Comment [KEYWORD]" held to the end. Clean bright look, crossfades only between sections.

## Tips the creators shared in these reels

- **Leave the bad takes in.** Hand over the raw footage, and the edit picks the good takes.
- **Give style notes in one plain sentence,** like "make it moody", "tighter cut" or "bigger captions". For revisions, add them as editor notes.
- **Review before you publish.** These creators look at a storyboard first. Here, Claude's draft goes to **editor check**, and fixes go back to Claude the same way.
- **Batch a week of reels from one shoot** by filming several setups in one session.
- **Show proof on screen:** a timecode, the raw length versus the final length, the editing app.
- **End on a one-word comment keyword** that triggers a DM automation, and pin the same instruction as a title.
