Use with podcast-clip and talking-head when the footage is one locked-off 4K wide of a person on a sofa or a podcast set. Measured on the Sara naturopathe edits (October 2026), which the studio made as its reference for this look.

**Three framings from one camera** (the face found per take, eyes in the upper part of the frame)
- Wide (W): zoom 1.0, the whole set. Medium (M): zoom 1.25, centred halfway between the face and the frame's middle, the face at about 40% of the height. Close (C): zoom 1.45 on the face, eyes at about 45%. Tighter than 1.5 crops the hair and puts titles over the face.
- Change framing at each phrase or sentence (every 2-6 s), never two cuts in a row at the same size. Every held shot pushes slowly (+3.5% over 6 s).
- Emphasis zooms on the key line: +20-28% in 0.3-0.35 s toward the face, hold 0.9-1.3 s, back in 0.5 s; at most one per 10-15 s.

**Whip-zoom into each new section** (a new step, a new idea)
- The outgoing framing accelerates in by 24% over its last 0.2 s; the next one lands from 20% closer over 0.3 s; a 0.1 white lift on the cut frame; a soft whoosh 0.12 s before. Rendered with motion blur (6 subframes), it reads as one camera move. Hard cuts everywhere else.

**B-roll cutaways**
- On the word they show, 0.9-1.7 s each, the voice running on and the captions staying up. Entry: a quick scale-in from 1.16 to 1.0 in 0.22 s with a whoosh; exit: a hard cut back to her. One every 8-12 s at most, never over the hook or the call to action.
- In a spoken list (« ménopause, manque d'énergie, sommeil, troubles digestifs »), one cutaway per item (0.95-1.1 s), with each item also as a pill.

**Graphics that carry the structure**
- A step tracker at the top (three pills with numbers; the current step in the accent colour, done steps ticked) for a method in steps; it ends where the call to action starts.
- Counters for the client's numbers (1 h 30, +7 jours, 12 semaines, 15 ans, 35 ans, −30 lb), one card at a time under the tracker, 1.6-2 s, a pop on landing; a counting number gets a roll of clicks.
- A strike-through for a correction (« RÉGIME » struck in red, then « STRUCTURE ALIMENTAIRE » in green) with a small vinyl scratch.
- A playful sticker for a local expression (« crise de bacon » 😤) with a spring sound and a stronger zoom.
- A name card the first time she appears (her name and « Naturopathe · 15 ans d'expérience »), lower left, 3 s.
- Titles and hero lines sit in the band above her head (y 220-480 on 1080 x 1920) and always fit on one line.

**The end card**: the picture blurs and darkens behind a box with the keyword (« Écris NATURE en commentaire »), held 1.5-2 s after the last word, the music fading under it.
