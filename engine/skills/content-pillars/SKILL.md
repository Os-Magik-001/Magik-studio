**What a pillar is**
- A theme the brand can talk about for a year, tied to a goal. Each pillar gets a name, one sentence on why it exists, and 3 video ideas.
- 3 to 5 pillars. A good mix for most businesses: about 40% value (teach, show how), 30% behind the scenes and people, 20% proof (results, reviews, numbers), 10% offer (products, promotions, events).

**Series beat one-offs**
- Give each pillar a repeatable format with a recognisable opening: "Before you wake up" (the 4 a.m. ritual), "Butter science" (one question, answered in 30 s), "Meet the regulars". Series build habits and are faster to shoot and edit.
- A series idea names its hook, its structure and how often it runs.

**Ideas that work**
- Every idea must be filmable by the client's team in their own place, in about two hours.
- Write the idea as a title the viewer would want to watch, not as a topic: "Why our croissant has 27 layers" beats "Croissant quality".
- Prefer ideas with a visual payoff (the crack of the crust, the reveal, the before and after).
- Never repeat an idea the studio already made for the client; build on what worked.
