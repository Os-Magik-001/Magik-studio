# Series episode prompts (The Boyz)

Each recipe has two parts:

- **Film:** what to shoot.
- **Notes for Claude:** paste this into **Launch the edit** on the video page, with the format set to Social short, 9:16.

The engine loads `SKILL.md` whenever the notes mention an episode, a series or The Boyz. These prompts are for the team; the engine doesn't load them.

Swap the bracketed parts for your own.

---

## Film for every episode
- **Scene:** frame it wide, on a tripod, with everyone in frame, and leave headroom above the heads for the logo. Shoot 4K if you can: the engine punches in on whoever speaks, so a 4K wide gives you close-ups for free.
- **Confessionals:** one person in the armchair in front of the bookshelf, framed waist-up, reacting to the scene. Shoot 2–3 short takes per character per episode.
- **Inserts:** 3–5 seconds each of an extreme close-up reaction, hands, a laptop screen, or a door opening.
- **Title sting:** shoot the group-shot intro once in 4K and drop it into every episode's footage. The engine uses 2s of it.
- **Sound:** a lav mic or a phone close to the speaker. The room echoes, and clean sound means accurate subtitles.

## 1. Scene episode
> The Boyz, episode [N]. Scene episode. Open on the line "[funniest or boldest line]" with its subtitle, then a 2s title sting with the drip logo "THE BOYZ / EPISODE [N]". English subtitles in the series look (translate mode). Cut all dead air, and punch in on whoever speaks. Cut to [character]'s confessional right after "[claim to contradict]". Snap-zoom with a hit on "[punchline]". Name labels on first appearance: [JOY BOY · the vision], [GHOST · the skills], [NAME · role]. End on "[cliffhanger line]" with "EPISODE [N+1] TOMORROW" and a follow pill @theboyz_journey. Under 40s.

## 2. New character or reveal
> The Boyz, episode [N]. Reveal episode: [NAME] joins the crew. Hook on the door opening with "[first line]". Glitch transition into the reveal. Show "[NAME]" as a huge hero word with a "NEW CHARACTER" label under it, plus a HUD typewriter label: "PROFILE LOADED · NAME: [NAME] · TRAIT: [trait] · STATUS: CONFIRMED". Then everyone's reactions, and end on the drip logo with "[NAME] JOINS THE CREW." English subtitles. 30–40s.

## 3. Mood or filler
> The Boyz filler episode. No dialogue. Moody music at high volume over the [scene] footage. Two text lines, each held 2–3s: "[setup line]", then "[payoff line]". Captions off. 20–30s.

## 4. Announcement (app launch, investor, big news)
> The Boyz, episode [N]. Announcement: [what]. Hook with the announcement line. A glitch on the reveal, with the key word as a hero title ("LAUNCH"). Cut to confessionals of each member reacting in one line each, 2–3s per member. End on a teaser label "[date / next step]" and a follow pill. English subtitles. Under 40s.
