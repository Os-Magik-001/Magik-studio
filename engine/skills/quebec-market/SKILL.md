**Language**
- Most of the studio's clients sell in Québec. French comes first: spoken lines, captions and words on screen in French unless the client says otherwise; English subtitles when the client also wants anglophones.
- When a video is bilingual, French leads and is at least as prominent as English. Check with the client when unsure.
- Write Québec French, not France French: natural, warm, conversational. Choose "tu" or "vous" from the brand's tone and keep it the same in every video.
- Spell out what an anglophone or a tourist might not know only when it helps the story.

**Local references**
- Neighbourhoods, streets, the metro, the seasons and the weather are strong hooks for local businesses ("Il fait -25 au Plateau…").
- Show the place: the storefront, the street, the regulars. Local proof beats generic stock moments.

**Calendar moments worth planning for**
- Saint-Valentin (Feb 14), the sugar shack season (March–April), Mother's and Father's Day (May, June), the Fête nationale (June 24), Canada Day and moving day (July 1), back to school (late August), Thanksgiving (2nd Monday of October), Halloween, Black Friday, the holidays and New Year.
- Plan seasonal videos 2 to 3 weeks before the date, so they are shot, approved and posted in time.
