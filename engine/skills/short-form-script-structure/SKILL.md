# Short-form script structure

## The eight blocks (from @robin_linkerpro, Oct 2026)
« Hook, lead, body, open loop, body, open loop, body, CTA. This is the perfect structure for short-form content. »
1. **Hook** — breaks the pattern of what they are scrolling: a surprising line, a contradiction, a strong picture in the first second. Not a greeting, not a name.
2. **Lead** — pulls them to the 10-second mark. Once they are anchored there, they don't scroll off. The lead promises what they will get (« voici les 3… », « je vais te montrer… »).
3. **Body 1** — the first piece of value, concrete.
4. **Open loop** — one line that creates curiosity: « but that's not the craziest part ». Now they have to stay to close it.
5. **Body 2**, 6. **Open loop**, 7. **Body 3** — the structure repeated twice, then one last body.
8. **CTA** — takes the warmest viewers and moves them to the next step of the funnel (book, comment a word, save, follow). One call to action only.

## Finding the blocks in raw footage
- Transcribe every take with word times. Mark the candidates: the most surprising sentence (hook), the line that announces the content (lead), each concrete fact or tip (bodies), every sentence that points forward (open loops), the ask (CTA).
- The hook can come from later in the talk (cold open), but only a full sentence, cut at word boundaries, and the person on camera must be the one saying it (lip check).
- Reach the first body by 10 s. Each body 4–8 s. Total 25–45 s for organic reels, under 30 s for ads.
- Never invent a line the person did not say. When the footage has no open loop, the edit can make one with a card (« et le meilleur, c'est le résultat ») or a visual question mark, never with a fake voice.

## Open loops in French
« Mais attends, c'est pas le plus fou. » · « Et c'est là que ça devient intéressant. » · « Mais le vrai secret, c'est autre chose. » · « Reste jusqu'à la fin, je te montre le résultat. » · « Et le dernier, personne n'en parle. » Use them in scripts the client records; in edits, find the person's own version.

## Other measured structures
- **The repeated example** (@tomfavre): one formula repeated four times (« X et Y, c'est joli, mais ajoute Z → résultat »), 5.5 s each, then a one-line conclusion and « enregistre ce post et partage-le ». The repetition is the retention device (info-overlays-on-the-word).
- **Three lines, three worlds** (@aslaniedits, 8 s): « You are growing » / « Your profits aren't » / « More customers mean more tasks… more operational costs » — each line its own colour world (blue, red, green, back to blue), the problem stated in 8 s, the solution left for the next video.

## How the edit shows the structure
- The structure can be shown: a counter or progress segments (one-world-explainer), a numbered block per section.
- Transitions mark block changes only (a body to an open loop), never inside a block.
- The biggest motion moment sits on the last body or the payoff, before the CTA (motion-budget).
