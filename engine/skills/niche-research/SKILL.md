**What to look for in the reels you watch**
- Patterns that repeat across several top reels, not one-offs. Say how many of the reels use each pattern ("6 of 10 open on the product in close-up").
- The numbers that explain reach: plays, likes, comments, length, cuts per second, words per minute, how fast the first word comes.
- Formats: talking head, voice-over montage, POV, tutorial, before/after, day in the life, street interview, trend sound. Which ones win in this niche.
- What people say in the comments that hints at questions the client could answer.

**Turn it into key points**
- Hooks: the pattern, a real example word for word, and why it works.
- Structures: the beats with their timing, and the typical length.
- Editing, text on screen, sound: concrete and measurable ("captions 3–5 words, bottom third, white with a yellow key word").
- Scripting notes: how the people talk (tone, pace, language).
- Ideas: video ideas for this client that use the patterns, adapted to their place and product.
- Avoid: what flops or feels overdone in the niche.

**Gaps are gold**
- Name what the top accounts don't do that this client can (a process nobody shows, a local angle, a person with personality).
- Note local versus global: what works for Montréal accounts may differ from the US accounts in the same niche.
