name: video-brief
description: What a video brief must hold so the scriptwriter, the videographer and the editor can all work from it
agents: planner, scriptwriter
---

A good brief answers, in 3 to 6 sentences:
1. **The goal**: what this video must do (bookings, pre-orders, follows, trust) and for whom.
2. **The message**: the one thing the viewer remembers.
3. **The hook idea**: what happens in the first two seconds.
4. **What we must see**: the product, the place, the people, the process, the proof.
5. **The call to action**: one, specific ("DM BRAND", "pre-order by Dec 20", "come Saturday before 10").
6. **Practical**: length (15–45 s for Reels), language, where it's filmed and with whom, anything to avoid.

Write it so a new videographer could read it once and know what the day is about. No jargon, no lists of hashtags.
