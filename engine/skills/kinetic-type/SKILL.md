# Kinetic type

Frames (f) are at 30 fps. Where a number comes from studio practice rather than a measured reference, it says so.

**Sync**
- Text starts on the onset of the word it shows and is readable within 3 f. Text that waits for the word reads as late; text a word early reads as a spoiler.
- Lyrics and rap: one word group every 0.4–0.6 s, each on its sung syllable, never on a metronome grid (measured on @slateacademy_).

**Entries**
- Caption words: 3–5 f, a short rise (10–14 px) out of a light blur.
- Titles: 8–12 f. The hero word: 10–14 f. 3D letters: 0.9 s in all, each letter rising out of depth.
- Curves: entries ease out (expo-out); exits ease in and take 60–70% of the entry's length. Never linear.

**Stagger**
- Letters 1–2 f apart (30–55 ms: the @aslaniedits big word lands its letters 55 ms apart).
- Words that are not spoken (a title, a label): 4–6 f apart. A whole build is done within 0.5 s.

**Overshoot and slam**
- The payoff word only: from 1.5× and blurred to 0.97, then 1.0, within 4–6 f. One overshoot, never a second bounce.
- One slam per reel: the word lands with a camera knock of 8–14 px decaying over 6–8 f, and a low impact under it (the approved Dubai reel).

**Blur and motion blur**
- A blur-in starts at about 8% of the cap height and is sharp on the landing frame.
- Moving text gets motion blur: 4 subframes in 2D, 8 in 3D.

**Glow**
- One word per stack, in the accent colour: a radius of 20–35% of the cap height, 40–70% opacity, in three layers (tight and bright, medium, wide and soft). It may light up letter by letter over 6–10 f (the approved AD1 punchline).
- Never on captions or labels.

**Holds**
- Hero words: 0.8–1.5 s. Numbers: 0.5–1.0 s.
- Unspoken text: at least (characters ÷ 15) + 0.5 s on screen, so it can be read.
- A stack or a title ends with its shot: never across a cut.

**Exits**
- On the cut when the next shot has its own text; otherwise over 4–6 f with a little blur.

**Stacks (the @aslaniedits model)**
- A pill word in a brush script, then a white line with one bold italic word, then the big glowing word, each appearing as it is said.
- Pixel blocks flicker on held frames (12 fps) for 0.15–0.35 s around the pill and the big word. A white swoosh draws under the line over 0.5 s and flies off.
- When the next few words are said while the stack holds, they become a small fourth line under the big word.
