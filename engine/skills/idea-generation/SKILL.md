---
name: idea-generation
description: Turning what works in a niche into original video ideas for one client, without copying anyone
agents: researcher, planner, scriptwriter
---

- Start from a pattern that works (a hook, a structure, a format), then make it this client's: their product, their place, their people, their language.
- For each pattern, find at least two angles and keep the one the client can film best.
- An idea is ready when you can say its hook in one line and its payoff in another.
- Prefer ideas with a visual payoff, a number, a transformation or a strong character.
- Change at least two of the reference's three parts (idea, structure, execution). Never reuse someone's words, jokes or exact setup.
- Check it against the brand's do's and don'ts and against the videos already made for the client.
