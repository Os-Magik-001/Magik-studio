Use this when the brief, script or editor's notes name where the video will run. The script's own target length still wins when it fits the platform.

**Length limits**

| Where it runs | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reel | up to 180s | Organic sweet spot is 15–35s |
| Instagram Reel ad | up to 60s | |
| TikTok feed | up to 10 min | Organic sweet spot is 15–35s |
| TikTok in-feed ad | 5–60s | 9–15s performs best |
| YouTube Shorts | up to 180s | |
| YouTube Shorts ad | 6–60s | |
| YouTube skippable ad | 12s or more | Brand and product must land before the skip button at 5s |
| YouTube non-skippable ad | 15–20s | |
| YouTube bumper | 6s maximum | 16:9 |
| Facebook Reel | up to 90s | |
| Snapchat ad | 3–180s | 3–5s performs best |
| Snapchat Commercial | 3–6s | Non-skippable |
| X video ad | up to 140s | 15s recommended |
| WhatsApp Status | 30s maximum | |

**Vertical video sits under the app's interface**
- On Reels, TikTok and Shorts, the top ~11% (the header) and the bottom ~23% (caption, audio row, buttons) are covered. A column of buttons also runs down the right edge in the lower half.
- The engine keeps captions and titles clear of these areas automatically. Your job is the picture: set each shot's `focus` so faces, products and the key action sit between about 12% and 75% of the frame height, not at the very bottom.
- YouTube Shorts crops the top and bottom in some views, so keep the subject centered.

**No-text placements**
- If the brief says Spotify Canvas, or another "no text" placement: no captions, no titles, no end card, a 3–8s loop, and no talking footage.
