Talking-head shoots often come back without B-roll. Generated cutaways fill the gap when they show objects, food, places or nature, never people: generated faces, hands and bodies read as fake (the studio's clients sent a generated group shot back for looking "so much like AI").

**What to generate**
- One cutaway per concrete noun the speaker says that a viewer can picture: coffee pouring for « un café de plus », a bedside clock at night for « sommeil », fermented foods for « digestion », whole foods for « structure alimentaire », a notebook and pen for « plan de match », a forest path for a journey of « 12 semaines », herbal tea and apothecary jars for a naturopath.
- 5 to 9 clips for three videos is enough; each one can serve several edits.

**How (Higgsfield, Kling 4.0, text to video)**
- 9:16, 1080p, 5 s (5 credits each). Prompt: the subject and action, then « photorealistic, shot on a cinema camera, natural colors, shallow depth of field, soft natural light, no people, no faces, no text ».
- Check each clip on a contact sheet before using it: a clip can contain its own cut (the coffee clip jumped to an extreme close-up halfway); use one continuous stretch.
- Ask for slow camera moves (a slow push, a slide); fast moves smear.

**How to cut them in**: see podcast-motion (0.9-1.7 s on the word, scale-in entry, the voice and captions running on). Tell the client in plain words that these are illustration shots, without naming tools in client documents.
