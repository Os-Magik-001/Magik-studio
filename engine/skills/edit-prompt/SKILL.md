Write the edit prompt like an experienced editor's brief, in this order:
1. **Structure**: the beats with target timings and the total length.
2. **Hook**: the first frame, the first line, the hook title and when it appears (from 0.0 s).
3. **Pacing**: how often something changes (a number: "a change every 2–3 s, faster in the first 3 s"), where to slow down and hold.
4. **Captions**: the preset, the language, the key words to highlight and their color from the brand kit.
5. **Motion moments**: each one with its template, the exact words or numbers, when it starts and how long it holds.
6. **Transitions**: only at changes of place or time; cuts everywhere else.
7. **Music and sound**: the mood, how loud under speech, where the drop or the ending lands, the sound effects that matter.
8. **Ending**: the call to action on screen, the end card or a loop.

Use numbers, not adjectives. Name the takes or shots to favour when the shoot plan asked for several. Respect the brand kit's style rules; the brand always wins over taste.
