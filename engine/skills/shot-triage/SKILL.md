name: shot-triage
description: Judging which footage is usable from the contact sheets, and trimming to the good part
formats: all
---

Before choosing shots, go through each clip's frames and judge what's usable.

**Reject or trim around these**
- Blur or missed focus, heavy shake, blown-out highlights, crushed blacks.
- A finger or object blocking the lens, an empty frame with no subject, a frame caught mid-whip-pan.
- Faces cut off by the frame edge, the subject walking out of frame.
- Burned-in title cards, other people's watermarks, letterbox bars.
- If burned-in subtitles fill the bottom ~15% of a source, crop them out with `focus` and a small punch-in, or skip the shot.

**Use only the good seconds**
- A 12s clip usually holds one strong 2–4s moment. Take that window, not the whole clip.
- Cut before an action finishes, or on a look, not on the subject wandering off.
- Never end a shot on a frame where nothing moves, unless it's a deliberate hold.

**Phone footage habits**
- Skip the first 0.5–1.5s: the phone is still being raised and steadied.
- If speech starts later than 2s in, start about 0.5s before it.
- Cut out "okay, recording", "alright" and similar starts and ends unless they're part of the story.
- Stop silent B-roll before it overstays: 8–10s at most, usually far less.

**Avoid repetition**
- Two shots that look almost the same add nothing; keep the better one.
- Don't put two shots of the same subject at the same size back to back. Change the size, the angle or the subject.
- Watch for repetition in playback order, not source order. Two similar lines or shots far apart in the footage can end up side by side after cutting.
- Break up a run of shots with the same color palette within about 4 shots.
- Avoid fast alternation between very bright and very dark shots.

**When the best shot is short**
- Hold it a little longer, or slow it slightly if the frame rate allows, rather than cutting to a weaker shot.
- If footage for a scripted beat is missing or unusable, say so in `open_questions`. Don't fill the gap with a bad shot.
