---
name: story-structures
description: Structures that keep people watching short videos, with beat timings for 15, 30 and 45 seconds
agents: scriptwriter, planner
---

**Pick one structure per video**
- **Promise → proof → payoff**: hook (0–2 s), 3 quick proofs (each 3–5 s), payoff and CTA (last 3 s).
- **Problem → solution**: the pain in the viewer's words, the turn ("so we did this"), the result.
- **Before → after**: the transformation, with the after teased first.
- **Process** (how it's made): 4–6 steps, each a clear visual change, ending on the finished product.
- **Countdown / list**: "3 things…", numbered on screen, the best one last.
- **Story loop**: open a question in the first line, answer it in the last; ideal for people and behind the scenes.
- **Day in the life / POV**: time stamps on screen move the story forward.

**Timing**
- 15 s: hook 2 s, 3 beats of 3–4 s, payoff 2 s.
- 30 s: hook 2 s, 5–6 beats of 4 s, payoff and CTA 3–4 s.
- 45 s: same, with one re-hook around the middle ("but here's the thing…").
- Something changes on screen every 2–4 s. No beat longer than 6 s without a new shot, text or angle.

**End strong**
- End on the payoff and one call to action. No long goodbyes.
- For loops, the last shot can echo the first.
