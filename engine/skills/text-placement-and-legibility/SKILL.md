# Text placement and legibility

**The interface (1080×1920)**
- Organic reels: keep text out of the top 220 px, the bottom 400 px, and the right 140 px from y 900 to 1700 (the like, comment and share column). Check against a real screenshot of the account when one is available.
- Ads: no text in the top 14% or the bottom 35% (the platform's ad guidance).
- Bands that work: titles at y 220–600 (or behind the head), captions at y 1150–1500. A caption under y 1540 is hidden by the username and caption text.

**Faces**
- No text over the box from the eyes to the chin, plus 8%. Check the first, middle and last frame of every text item: people move.
- Pointers and labels stay pinned to the screen and never chase a face (the owner, Oct 27).
- Marks around a head: the front one 0.5 face-widths to the left, the one behind the head 0.6 to the right. Any closer and the head hides it (Loadedfordays v2).

**Sizes and contrast**
- Caption cap height 48–64 px; nothing smaller than 28 px; labels at least 36 px.
- At least 4.5:1 contrast against the measured background. Otherwise a shadow (12–16 px blur, 60% black) or a 2–4 px dark stroke painted under the fill.
- Bright overcast or grass backgrounds: add a soft dark scrim behind the text block rather than darkening the video.

**How much at once**
- One title block, the captions and one tag. A stack replaces the caption while it is up.
- Behind-the-subject titles: 2–4 per reel, with the head covering at most 25–30% of the letters' height. No camera move while one is up (the head would slide over the words).
- Anything dropping toward a person starts at most 450 px above, or it crosses the face.
