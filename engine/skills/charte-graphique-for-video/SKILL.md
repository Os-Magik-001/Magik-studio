name: charte-graphique-for-video
description: Turning a brand into a visual identity that works on vertical video: colors, fonts, captions, motion, logo and tone
agents: strategist, motion
---

**Palette**
- 3 to 6 colors with roles: primary (the brand), accent (highlights, the key word in captions), background, text, and a secondary if needed.
- Keep the client's colors when they have them. Otherwise pick from what the camera will show (the shop, the product, the uniforms) so graphics and footage belong together.
- Text on video needs contrast: white or a very light color on footage, with the accent for one or two key words. Avoid mid-tones for text.

**Fonts**
- One headline font and one body font, from Google Fonts unless the client has their own. One expressive (serif or display), one plain sans. Never three.
- Bold, condensed or display fonts read best for short hooks; a clean sans for captions.

**Captions and motion**
- Choose the caption preset by niche and energy: bold_pop or karaoke_box for energetic and young, editorial or minimal for premium and calm, subtitle for talking heads in two languages.
- Describe the motion energy in one line (calm and warm, punchy and fast) and the templates that fit (title_3d, stat_card, kinetic_type, logo_reveal…).

**Logo, tone and rules**
- Logo: where it appears (end card, a small corner badge) and never over faces or the product.
- Tone of voice: three adjectives and two example lines in the client's language.
- Do's and don'ts: concrete and checkable ("never film the kitchen messy", "always show the product whole before it's cut").
