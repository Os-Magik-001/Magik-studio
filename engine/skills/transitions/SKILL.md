**Hard cuts are the edit.** In the studio's references (about 520 reels from seven sources), hard cuts are the large majority everywhere, and in dialogue comedy and series episodes they are all of them. A transition is a punctuation mark: it signals a change of place, set-up, section or energy. If you can't name what it signals, use a hard cut.

**Budget by format**
- **Dialogue comedy, series episodes, skits with dialogue:** hard cuts only. When the footage has an in-camera whip, a pan to the next speaker or a body crossing the lens, cut inside that movement; never add an effect.
- **Creator reels with several set-ups:** a short whip on most set-up, speaker or location changes (13 of 16 in the measured slower creators): `slide_left` 0.3 s, or `zoom_in` 0.3 s when the next shot is much wider or closer, with a soft `whoosh`. Hard cuts inside a set-up. A creator who cuts every 1-2 s hard-cuts every change.
- **Realtor and argument reels:** hard cuts, plus a 0.1-0.2 s `dip_to_white` on the turns of the argument ("but", claim to proof) and into and out of a photo insert: at most 3 per reel, about one per 15 s.
- **Meme skits:** hard cuts between the account's own shots; a 0.2-0.4 s `dip_to_black` into and out of any borrowed clip; 0.4-0.6 s dips between slow "ritual" steps when slowness is the joke; a 0.1-0.25 s black beat (contiguous segments of one take joined by `dip_to_black`) on each step of a rule of three or each item of a list meme.
- **Ads and promos:** one designed transition per change of place (a `zoom_in` push-through, or a whip as `slide_left`), hard cuts inside a scene. Crossfades and dips at real section changes don't count against that budget.
- **Documentary, interview and news pieces:** hard cuts, plus 0.5-1 s crossfades or dips only at act breaks. No slides, wipes or zooms.
- **Wordless motion pieces:** act changes as 0.3-0.4 s light sweeps or blooms (`dip_to_white`, `wipe_left`), or a hard cut on a colour flip; never slow 0.6 s dips.
- A cut used as a rhythm beat (on an action, a word or a hit) must be a true hard cut. Even a short dissolve blurs it.

**Use the footage's own movement first** (these read as one move and need no effect)
- **Camera whip pans:** end the outgoing shot inside its whip and start the incoming one inside a whip going the same way.
- **Something crossing the lens:** a door swinging open, a body or a car passing. Cut when it fills the frame.
- **A camera move into the next beat:** speed it up (speed 3-4, sound muted, a `whoosh`) and land at normal speed on the next line.
- **A cut on action:** a step, a turn, a hand reaching for a handle.

**Which one**
- **`crossfade`, 0.4-0.7 s:** time passing or a change of place, in calm pieces.
- **`dip_to_black`, 0.3-0.5 s:** a chapter break, or a breather before a new section; at most 2 per 30 s outside memes. It pairs well with a title on the next shot. Use 0.1 s just before a big hit in a montage.
- **`dip_to_white`, 0.1-0.2 s:** a flash on a true energy peak, a reveal, the hook's first cut (1-2 frames), or an argument turn in a realtor-style reel. More than 3 per reel looks amateur.
- **`slide_left` or `slide_up`, 0.25-0.35 s:** high energy into high energy, like a whip pan; the creator set-up change. Hold at least 0.7 s of each shot on either side. Use one direction for the whole video (`slide_left` reads as "forward"); never mix them.
- **`zoom_in`, 0.3-0.4 s:** a push through into the next place, or into a detail of what was just shown. Center it on a subject that matches the next shot's subject (a face to a face, a door to a car interior), with a `whoosh`. Only at changes of place: the studio's approved edits use zooms there and nowhere else.
- **`glitch`, 0.2-0.35 s:** a reveal, a new character, a "system" moment. At most 2 per episode, except in a reveal special, which may glitch on every cut on the beat.
- **`wipe_left` and `circle_open`:** novelty moves. Use them only when the brand style is explicitly playful, or as the sweep back to dark in a motion piece.

**Rules**
- Use one kind of transition per video, two at most (a meme: cut plus dip; a creator reel: cut plus whip, plus one hook flash).
- Never put a transition on a shot that is mid-push; let the move finish first. Never hide a moment the viewer should see under a transition.
- Give a transition air: the next line starts 0.1-0.25 s after it ends, never under it, and no word is clipped by it (the studio's clients sent cuts back for "more time between the transitions").
- No split edit under a designed transition.
- A transition overlaps both shots by its length. Keep it well under half of either shot.
- Before the end card: a hard cut, or a 0.3-0.5 s `crossfade` or `dip_to_black`.
- Never start the video with a transition.
