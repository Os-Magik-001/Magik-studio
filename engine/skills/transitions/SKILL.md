---
name: transitions
description: Which transition to use between two shots, and how long
formats: all
---

A hard cut is the default. Most cuts in a pro edit are hard cuts. Use a transition only when it says something.

**Choose by the energy of the two shots:**
- **Same scene, same energy:** hard cut.
- **A cut used as a rhythm beat** (on an action, a word or a hit) must be a true hard cut. Even a short dissolve blurs the beat.
- **A change of time or place, or a gentle mood shift:** `crossfade`, 0.4 to 0.6s.
- **A chapter break or a breather before a new section:** `dip_to_black`, 0.3 to 0.5s.
  - Use at most 2 per 30s.
  - It works best right before a title.
- **A bright, upbeat reveal:** `dip_to_white`, 0.15 to 0.25s. It reads like a flash.
- **High energy into high energy:** `slide_left` or `slide_up`, 0.25 to 0.35s, like a whip.
  - Hold at least 0.7s of each shot on either side.
  - Match the slide direction to the motion in the shots.
- **Moving into a detail of what was just shown:** `zoom_in`, 0.3 to 0.4s.
- **`wipe_left` and `circle_open`** are novelty moves. Use them only if the brand style calls for playful.

**Rules**
- Use one kind of transition between any two shots, never two stacked.
- Don't mix many transition kinds in one video. One or two kinds, used consistently, look intentional.
- A transition overlaps both shots by its length, so the shots need enough length to give. Keep the transition well under half of either shot.
- Before the end card: `crossfade` or `dip_to_black`, 0.3 to 0.5s, unless the brand style says hard cut.
