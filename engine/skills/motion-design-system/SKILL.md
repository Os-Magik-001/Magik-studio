name: motion-design-system
description: Designing a video's graphics as one system: concept, type, color, timing and where things sit
agents: motion
---

**One concept**
- Every graphic in the video serves one idea you can say in a sentence ("a warm notebook: hand-set serif titles, butter-gold underlines"). A video is designed when its graphics look like one family.

**Type and color**
- Two typefaces at most, from the house list; one accent color from the brand. Big type: hero words 140–180 px, titles 90–120 px, labels 36–44 px on a 1080 × 1920 frame.
- 3 to 6 words per title. One key word in the accent color, never a whole sentence.

**Timing**
- In: 0.25–0.4 s, ease-out. Hold: at least 1.2 s for every 3 words. Out: 0.2 s, or cut with the shot.
- Land graphics on the words they echo and on the music's beats where possible.
- One graphic at a time. Leave the payoff shot clean.

**Placement**
- Keep clear of the platform's interface: the top 210 px, the bottom 480 px and the right 150 px in the lower half.
- 90 px side margins. Nothing over faces or the product's key detail.

**Motion language**
- One family of moves for the whole video (all slide-ups, or all scale-ins). No glows, lens flares, particle bursts, shine sweeps or bouncy springs.
