# Minimal Glow Opener (logo-reveal / motion-template promo)

## When to use it
Use this when the brief is a brand opener, logo reveal, product teaser, template showcase or mood piece with no one speaking. The footage is abstract: dark B-roll, light leaks, product macros, UI or screen captures, logo stills. If the source has a talking head, this is the wrong skill. Pick a dialogue skill.

## What the reference shares (measured)
- Duration is 32.9s. Aim for 28-35s.
- 0.12 cuts/s, median shot 10.6s. That is about 4 real shots in the whole reel. Each long shot carries several text beats, and the text changes give the rhythm, not the cuts.
- Speech is 0% and audio is 100% original (an ambient/electronic bed). There is no voice and there are no captions.
- Palette is pure black backgrounds with one glowing accent (green, blue-violet, magenta) and thin white sans text. One section in the middle flips to white with black UI pills.
- A persistent bottom label reads "Project File In Description" (CTA as a quiet label, not a graphic).
- The reel ends on a centered, glowing logo held still.

## Hook (0-3s)
Open on near-black with one small bright point or element in the upper-middle third.
- Choose the darkest abstract clip.
- Set brightness -0.3 and contrast +0.3 so the background crushes to black.
- Saturation +0.2 makes the glow pop.
- Add a slow push from zoom 1.0 to 1.12 over the whole shot.
- At 1.5-2.0s, fade in the first phrase (2 words, e.g. "Finding form") centered just under the element: text style title, small size, animation fade.
- No sound effect at frame 0. Let a soft riser start at about 0.5s so the first phrase lands on its peak.

## Structure recipe (4 shots, 3 acts + payoff)
1. **Dark act, 0-11s.** One shot (or a held freeze if the clip is short).
   - 3 phrases, each on screen 2.5-3.5s: fade in 0.4s, hold, fade out 0.3s, then a 0.2-0.4s empty beat before the next.
   - Phrases are 2-3 word abstract statements ("Focus organizes motion").
   - Alternate the text color between white and the accent color.
2. **White act, about 11-21s.** Transition is dip_to_white (0.4-0.6s).
   - Use a bright, clean clip, or a hold frame pushed to brightness +0.4 and contrast +0.2.
   - The look stays natural so the white stays white.
   - Text switches to boxed style (dark pill on white) entering with slide_up or slide_left, one pill every 2.5-3s ("Create rhythm", "Shape meaning").
   - Slow push or a gentle reframe pan across the frame (focus point drifting left to right) mimics the line-drawing motion.
3. **Return to dark, about 21-28s.** Transition is dip_to_black (0.5s).
   - A colorful glow clip (blobs, bokeh, light leak) with saturation +0.3.
   - 1-2 phrases with the typewriter animation ("Thought in motion"), centered.
4. **Logo payoff, about 28-33s.** Transition is crossfade 0.6s or circle_open.
   - Use the logo still as a hold shot, centered, with zoom 1.0 to 1.06.
   - Use title_3d or hero text for the brand name (pop or fade), or just the logo card with no text.
   - Hold it at least 3s. End on the hold; do not cut to black early.

If there is less footage, use freeze frames (hold) to stretch a clip. Never fill the time with extra cuts.

## Pacing and cutting rules
- Make 3-5 cuts total. Never put a shot under 4s, except the logo, which can be 4-5s.
- No hard cuts. The only transitions are crossfade, dip_to_black, dip_to_white and circle_open, each 0.4-0.7s.
- Text beats every 2.5-3.5s create the rhythm. Use 8-10 text beats in a 33s reel.
- Leave 0.2-0.5s of empty frame between phrases. That breath is the style.
- Speed: play abstract clips at 0.5-0.75x to make motion floaty. Never speed up.
- Mute all clip audio. Only the music bed and SFX play.

## Framing
- Everything is centered. The hero element sits in the upper-middle third, and text sits 8-12% of frame height below it.
- Reframe on a focus point so the glowing element is centered, then let the slow push do the work.
- Keep only one point of interest in frame. If a clip is busy, punch in (zoom 1.4-1.8) on the cleanest area and darken the rest with brightness -0.2.
- Use the cinematic look for the dark acts and natural for the white act. Never use the vintage, warm or punchy looks.

## Text and captions
- **Auto captions:** off. There is no speech.
- **Phrase text:** title or label style, thin and small (size small), centered, 2-3 words, sentence case, no punctuation.
- **Animations:** fade (default), typewriter (one phrase per act at most), slide_up (white-act pills only). No pop on phrases.
- **Persistent CTA:** add a label at the bottom center (about 92% down), small, white on dark and black on white. Use text like "Link in description" or "Project file in description". It appears at about 11s (the white flip) and stays to the end. Use no comment_cta, follow_pill or emoji.
- Use only one emphasized word per phrase, colored with the accent.

## Motion templates
- title_3d for the brand name at the payoff only.
- Optional: a card for a floating UI screenshot in the white act, centered, entering with slide_up.
- Do not use character_intro, cast_lineup, stat_card, lower_third_3d, counter or progress_bar. They break the minimal tone.

## Music and sound
- Run one ambient or soft electronic bed at high volume (no dialogue to protect), starting at 0s from a soft intro point.
- Align each dip transition to a bar change.
- SFX are sparse, 3-5 total:
  - a riser into the first phrase
  - a whoosh on dip_to_white
  - a soft whoosh on dip_to_black
  - a ding (or hit at low volume) on the logo reveal
- Never put a pop or click on every text beat.

## Avoid
- Fast cuts, glitch, wipe, zoom_in transitions, shake.
- Bold or heavy fonts, all-caps headlines, karaoke or bold_pop captions.
- More than one accent color per act.
- Busy B-roll, faces, or visible real-world clutter.
- Music with vocals or drums that hit hard.
- Ending on black without the logo hold.

**Music and sound** (from the reference reels)
- Use a library music bed, not clip audio. First choice is meditating-beat (ambient-minimal, 92 BPM). Alternatives are blippy-trance (electronic-tech, medium energy, 100 BPM) for a techier brand and finally-see-the-light (luxury-elegant, 93 BPM) for a premium brand. Set volume high because there is no dialogue to dip under. Start the track at 0s from its intro (start point 0), so the opening point of light appears over near-silence that builds. Place the dip_to_white at about 11s and dip_to_black at about 21s on bar lines (at about 92 BPM, one bar is about 2.6s). Let the track run under the logo hold to the final frame with no cut-off. Layer sparse SFX at low volume: a riser into the first phrase, a whoosh on each dip, and a ding on the logo. Avoid hip-hop, funk, upbeat-pop and epic trailer tracks.
- Library moods: ambient-minimal, electronic-tech, luxury-elegant, atmospheric, airy, futuristic, calm, glowing, sleek.
