# Minimal Glow Opener (logo reveal / motion-template promo)

## When to use it
Use this when the brief is a brand opener, logo reveal, product teaser, template showcase or mood piece with no one speaking. The footage is abstract: dark B-roll, light leaks, product macros, UI captures, logo stills. If the source has a talking head, pick a dialogue skill.

## What the reference does (measured frame by frame, Oct 2026)
- **32.9 s.** Aim for 28–35 s.
- **No hard cuts at all.** Five sections: a 1.7 s intro, a 12.8 s dark act, a 7.9 s white act, a 7.6 s return to dark and a 2.9 s logo. The three act changes are designed transitions of **0.3–0.4 s** drawn inside the animation (a light band sweeping down at 1.67 s, a dot swelling into a white bloom at 14.47 s, a gradient sweeping in from the right at 22.37 s).
- **Inside an act, every phrase change is a morph, never a transition:** one element is always moving and turns into the next (dots form a ring, the ring becomes a rounded square, a star, a pill, a band, a dot). Nothing is still except the final logo.
- **Text rhythm: 11 text beats, one every 3.0 s.** Holds 1.0–3.2 s (median about 2.2 s; the dark-act phrases are short, 1.0–1.2 s). In the dark acts about 1 s of moving graphic with no text separates two phrases (0.9–1.3 s, 1.8 s before each act change); the white-act pills hand over in 0.2–0.3 s.
- **Audio:** an ambient bed, no voice, original audio. No clear beat grid: none of the act changes lands on a detected beat.
- **Palette:** pure black with one glowing accent per moment (cyan, blue, pink), thin white or grey sans; the middle act flips to white with black pills.

## Hook (0–2 s)
- Frame 0 is pure black; a thin line with a dot rises at 0.25 s.
- The first phrase arrives at **0.5 s** ("Let's begin"), small thin grey sans, high in the frame (about 17% from the top), `fade`.
- The first section change comes at 1.7 s: a 0.4 s light band sweeps down and wipes the phrase away.

## Structure recipe
1. **Intro, 0–1.7 s:** the single element and the first phrase.
2. **Dark act, 1.7–14.5 s:** the element keeps morphing; 4 phrases of 2–3 words ("With a system", "Finding form", "Form / Defines focus", "Focus organizes motion"), each held 1–2.5 s with about 1 s of graphic alone between them. One two-level title (a big white word over a smaller blue line).
3. **White act, 14.5–22.4 s:** a white bloom opens it. Black pills with a sparkle icon and white text drift in from the right or the top and leave to the left along a hand-drawn line; a new pill every 2.5–3 s; a colour gradient passes through the pill mid-hold (a highlight with no text change).
4. **Return to dark, 22.4–30 s:** a gradient sweep back to black. An outline pill draws itself and a word types in, then slides out left with speed streaks; glowing blobs carry the last phrase typed word by word ("Thought" / "in" / "motion", 0.5–1 s apart); the blobs play alone for about 1.5 s before the logo.
5. **Logo, 30–32.9 s:** the white mark centred, the URL fading in under it 0.5 s later, **held still** to the last frame. Do not end on black.

If there is less footage, stretch a clip with `hold` or speed 0.5–0.75x. Never fill the time with extra cuts.

## Pacing and cutting rules
- 3–4 section changes in total; no `cut` between sections.
- Transitions 0.3–0.4 s: `dip_to_white` for the flip to white, `wipe_left` for the sweep back to dark, a 0.3 s `crossfade` into the logo. Never slow 0.6 s dips.
- Inside a section, the change comes from the footage's own motion and from text; keep one shot per section where you can.
- A text beat every 3 s (11 in 33 s); phrase holds 1–3 s; leave about 1 s with no text between phrases in the dark acts.
- Speed: abstract clips at 0.5–0.75x to make motion floaty; never speed up.
- Mute all clip audio; only the bed and the effects play.

## Framing
- Everything centred; text in the middle band (47–52% of the height), except the opening phrase high in the frame.
- Reframe so the glowing element sits centred, then let a slow push work (1.0 → 1.1 over a section). The logo hold is static (zoom 1.0 to 1.0).
- One point of interest in frame. If a clip is busy, punch in (1.4–1.6) on its cleanest area and darken the rest (brightness -0.2).
- `cinematic` look for the dark acts; `natural` for the white act so the white stays white. Never `vintage`, `warm` or `punchy`.

## Text and captions
- **Auto captions off:** there is no speech.
- **Phrases:** `title` or `label`, thin and small, centred, 2–3 words, sentence case, no punctuation.
- **Animations:** `fade` by default, `typewriter` for the last act's phrase, `slide_up` or `slide_left` for the white-act pills (`boxed`, dark on white). No `pop` on phrases.
- **Persistent CTA label:** a small `label` at the bottom centre (about 88% down), black on white then white on black, from the white flip to the last frame ("Project file in description", "Link in bio"). No comment_cta, follow_pill or emoji.
- One emphasised word per phrase at most, in the accent colour.

## Motion templates
- `logo_reveal` or `title_3d` for the brand at the payoff only, held still.
- Optional: a `card` for a floating UI screenshot in the white act, entering with `slide_up`.
- No character_intro, cast_lineup, stat_card, lower_third_3d, counter or progress_bar.

## Music and sound
- One ambient or soft electronic bed at volume `high` (no dialogue to dip under), starting at 0 s from a soft intro, running under the logo to the last frame.
- Put the act changes where the bed changes (a new layer, a swell), not on a forced bar grid.
- Effects, 3–5 in total: a soft whoosh on each sweep or bloom, a `ding` (or a quiet `hit`) on the logo. Never a pop or click on every text beat.

## Avoid
- Hard cuts, glitch, zoom_in transitions, shake.
- Bold or heavy fonts, all-caps headlines, bold_pop or karaoke captions.
- More than one accent colour at a time; busy B-roll, faces, real-world clutter.
- Music with vocals or hard drums.
- Ending on black, or pushing on the logo.

**Music and sound** (from the reference reels)
- Use a library bed, not clip audio: meditating-beat (ambient-minimal, 92 BPM) first; blippy-trance (electronic-tech, 100 BPM) for a techier brand; finally-see-the-light (luxury-elegant, 93 BPM) for a premium brand. Volume `high`, start point 0 so the opening point of light appears over near-silence that builds. Layer sparse effects at low gain: a whoosh on each act change and a ding on the logo.
- Library moods: ambient-minimal, electronic-tech, luxury-elegant, atmospheric, airy, futuristic, calm, glowing, sleek.
