# @j.k.hamington: what the event and nightlife promos teach the edit engine

**Covered:** all 72 reels up to 150 s posted 2023-03-20 to 2026-10-22 (41.7 min; 2 longer reels left out), measured automatically. 17 of them are The Boyz episodes posted as collabs; they are counted in The Boyz report only, which leaves 55 of J.K.'s own reels. The 20 most played of those (1.1k to 23k plays) were watched frame by frame (hook at 4 fps, the whole reel at 2 fps, 8 frames around every cut).

**What the account is:** the personal account of the founder of Platinum Game (Instagram management, event and nightlife marketing in Montreal and Toronto). Of the 20 watched: 8 event, party and venue montages, 6 single-take or locked-off pieces (an outfit-change skit cut twice, a cosplay, a runway, a photo shoot, a gym meme), 4 voice-over promos (app teasers, an event invitation, a fan-film trailer) and 2 on-camera talks (a two-presenter "news" promo, an interview). 14 have no talking; most play a licensed song. Plays are low and close together, so the counts below say "what this house does" more than "what wins".

## Rules for the engine (event, nightlife and personal-brand promos)
1. **Choose the cut grid from the song before choosing shots, one mode per section:** bar mode (every segment a whole number of bars, the hero shot 2 bars, equal lengths fine when subject and size change) for a clear 4/4 track and steady footage; free mode (0.45-1.8 s, median 0.75-1.15 s, no three in a row within 0.1 s, each cut on an action) for phone footage. Never a fixed hold with no musical value.
2. **The single biggest change on a phrase onset** (within about 0.05 s): the first sound of a sung line, the drop or the hook word; the first full-frame shot on the drop.
3. **Event recap order:** the name in the first 1.2 s; people alternating with things and rooms, size changing on every cut; the host in the first 3 s and in the last shot; the last shot the longest (2.0-2.3 s). For 30-45 s: intro shots of 1.6-3.5 s, one 1.9-2.0 s logo or title card as the breath, the grid, a faster run of 1.0-1.2 s into the CTA.
4. **Cut on the footage's own cover and moves:** end on the frame of maximum cover when something fills 70% of the frame; cut inside whips; never cut a reveal by pan, tilt or pull-back (keep 1.5-3.4 s).
5. **Single-action shots may run up to 11 s** when the hook text says what to wait for; end with a hard cut on the payoff, to a 0.5-0.6 s black card or a 2.5-3 s reaction.
6. **List and transformation formats:** one segment per item (5.3-6.0 s) cut on a cover action, one typed `on_screen_text` per item (0.25-0.4 s after the cut), the last item held 6-7 s, the text written as a joke with a payoff.
7. **`speed` 1.0.**
8. **Transitions:** hard cuts inside sections; at most one designed transition per section change and 3 per video (`zoom_in` 0.3-0.4 s hook to body and into the end card, `glitch` 0.3-0.5 s on a logo reveal); 0.1-0.3 s black blinks between POV arrival moments; a `crossfade` of 0.5-0.6 s only into the last shot or out of a logo card; never more than 0.1 s of black at the start.
9. **Text:** no captions in a montage; a hook line from 0.0 s (3-8 words, a scarcity or call-out line plus the event name), removed 0.3-0.6 s after the first cut; no text until the CTA; 1-3 CTA cards of 2.0-3.0 s, the last the flyer.
10. **Voice-over montages:** the new segment starts 0.0-0.15 s before each section's phrase; B-roll of 0.9-1.9 s may change inside words during a continuous read; a 1 s gap filled with three 0.4-0.6 s shots or a logo card; key phrases on screen word by word.
11. **Talking parts:** the engine's handles reproduce the measured turns; hold an interjection 0.5-0.6 s after its last word; with two cameras, change angle every 3-6 s in a pause; for a long interview cut-down, open on the payoff line (3 s or less).
12. **Stills:** never 4 s or more in the body; a still only as an end card (2.5-3 s), or with a push {1.0, 1.05}.

## Against the current skills
- **edit-styles "never the same shot length three times in a row":** broken on purpose in 5 of 20 (bar grids, list formats). Right for free cutting and talk; allow equal lengths when each is a whole number of bars or the video is one item per shot.
- **trailer-and-montage "no shot over 6 s except the final hold" and "no scene over 40% of the runtime":** right for multi-shot montages, wrong for one complete action with a payoff and for transformation and outfit-change formats.
- **transitions "before the end card: a hard cut, a crossfade or a dip":** 3 of 8 end cards come in with a zoom blur or a glitch, the house look of the two top reels; allow it when the brand uses it. "Never start with a transition": right (0.1 s at most). "`dip_to_black` at most 2 per 30 s": allow 0.1-0.3 s blinks in a POV arrival sequence.
- **trailer-and-montage "a line before a hit ends 0.2-0.6 s before the cut":** with voice-over these reels cut within 0.0-0.15 s of the phrase.
- **edit-styles Punchy "0.8-1.5 s" B-roll:** party recaps go down to 0.45 s.
- **The trim pass** (pauses over 0.75 s): the interview keeps pauses of 0.76-1.13 s, but it is the weakest reel; 0.75 s is right for social cut-downs.
- **Confirmed:** hard cuts are the edit, never reuse a shot, cut when something crosses the lens, every cut changes something, end cards of 2-3 s.

## What the engine can't do yet
- Light leaks and film burns, RGB light-streak flares, mirror and kaleidoscope effects, a true zoom or motion blur, a logo slam and a shatter wipe, giant CTA words zooming down to size, a typing search bar, a glitching game title screen.
- Split-screen panels (only as three `card` overlays), letterboxed 16:9 footage with titles in the bands, a phone mock-up playing app screens.
- Finding in-camera cut points (jacket throws, whips, people crossing the lens): it needs a "frame covered / maximum blur" finder.
- Reliable bar grids and lyric-timed cuts: tempo estimates disagree on some tracks, and speech recognition hears lyrics as speech.
- Borrowed material (a Kobe reaction clip, 50 Cent footage, TV news audio).
