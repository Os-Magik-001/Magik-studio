# @themarketinginterns: what the meme skits teach the edit engine

**Covered:** all 208 reels up to 150 s posted 2026-08-12 to 2026-10-23 (68.9 min; 2 longer reels left out), measured automatically. The 25 most played (0.57 to 14.4 million plays) were watched frame by frame (hook at 4 fps, the whole reel at 2 fps, 8 frames around every cut).

**What the account is:** 22 of the 25 top reels are no-dialogue meme skits: one line of text on screen from frame 0, a song or trending sound as the whole soundtrack, and 2 or 3 locked-off shots of the cast in the office. The other 3 are a narrated office tour (Db9Q-vyPZGJ), a one-take handheld tour (DeQgB-hACCK) and a lip-sync to a viral dialogue sound (DeoIqoRgPRQ). So the engine's dialogue rules mostly don't apply here, and its "meme post" recipe needs rewriting.

## How the top 25 are cut

### Few cuts, a late turn, a long payoff
- **Shots made by the editor:** median 3 per reel in ranks 1-12 (2, 9, 6, 4, 3, 2, 3, 12, 2, 14, 2, 2) and a median of 1 cut in ranks 13-25; 3 reels have no cut at all (11.1, 15.6 and 24.8 s). The account's own cuts come at about 0.18-0.3 per second; the rest of the measured cuts live inside borrowed clips.
- **No cut in the first 2.5 s** in 21 of 25 (median first cut 2.8-2.95 s). The hook is a held, composed wide with the premise text already on screen.
- **The turn sits at 37-48% of the reel** (median 44%) and the payoff is the longer part: setup 2.7-5.9 s (median 4.3 s), payoff 3.7-8.7 s (median 5.3 s), 38-73% of the reel. Examples: DcYNO8hgzUS turns at 48%, Dds0y9pA33s at 44%, DeGDrsdgzv6 at 37%.
- **The first cut is the twist.** It lands on the frame the twist text appears (DdtMitiBCcc @ 2.77 "opus 5.5", DdXe1EDPAZW @ 4.27 "vs. night"), or 0.8-1.3 s after it so the cast reacts first (DeLEWW_JM4L: text at 5.0, cut at 6.10; Dd0oh0jgFJF: text at 2.0, laptop tossed at 3.0, cut at 3.27).

### What the cuts land on
- **A movement (6 of 12 in the first half):** an exit while the last 2-3 people are still leaving (DcYNO8hgzUS @ 4.90, DeBitgrg8hV @ 3.30); the peak of a pose matched to a film clip of the same pose (DdiXX1Pgsxz @ 2.73); a phone lifted, cut straight to its screen (DeGDrsdgzv6 @ 5.17); typing hands (DeqVx3kgFmD @ 0.70).
- **A sound event (4 of 12):** the voice onset within 22 ms with the text swapped on the same frame (Dev8DBWAdX7 @ 4.53, "What my mom thinks I do:" → "What I actually do:"); the last sound of a phrase within 4 ms (DeGDrsdgzv6 @ 10.17); the music returning after its only gap (DeTbPTcA5UN @ 4.27); a lyric gap.
- **On-screen typing reaching the joke** (DeqVx3kgFmD @ 4.80, DeLu5PTAKhL @ 14.2).
- **Not on the beat.** The share of cuts on a beat is at or below chance (for example 0.17 against 0.22, 0.0 against 0.13, 0.08 against 0.26); only the numbered phase montage is cut to the beat (DeIbCoSPmkX, 3 of 4, offset 0.022 s). Snapping meme cuts to beats would pull them off the exits and gestures that make them work.

### Camera and sizes
- **Locked-off wides** in 22 of 25; 3 shot from a high ceiling corner like a security camera (DcYNO8hgzUS, DeBitgrg8hV, DeTbPTcA5UN). The gags are 8-10 bodies moving at once, which only reads in the full wide.
- **No jump cuts, punch-ins, zooms or speed changes** in any of the 25. Close inserts (hands, screen) in 3 (DeqVx3kgFmD, DeLu5PTAKhL, the tour).

### Borrowed clips and templates
- **12 of 25 pay off with a borrowed meme or film clip**, shown uncropped on black rather than reframed to vertical (DeqVx3kgFmD @ 5.9-13.2, Dds0y9pA33s @ 3.5, DdiXX1Pgsxz @ 3.0). The plate-then-fire reaction clip is a ready-made edit dropped in whole: its internal cuts fall 0.40/0.83/1.60/3.06/3.80 s after its start in both Dd0oh0jgFJF and DdvU3y5A4qi.
- **Templates are reused:** ranks 4 and 6 share the sound and the payoff clip (only the setup length, 3.5 against 2.9 s, and the text change); ranks 1 and 9 share the wake-up premise; 4 of the second half re-shoot an earlier reel in a new place (DeLEWW_JM4L is Ddf6-oBAPvR again, both over 1.3 M plays).

### Reactions and timing
- **The reaction is the punchline**, held 1.5-5.4 s: DeLu5PTAKhL 2.8 s then 3.8 s; Dds0y9pA33s 4.4 s; a ping-pong of insert and cast at 0.7-1.0 s per shot ending on a pose held 1.3-1.4 s (DeLEWW_JM4L @ 6.10-10.2).
- When the cast reacts inside the wide, the reaction starts 0.8-2.0 s after the text line appears and runs 0.5-1.6 s before the cut.
- **The "pause before the punchline" is the silent setup** (2.7-5.9 s), helped by a line cut off mid-word or a question ("lim-", "What is the intern doing bro").
- **Rule of three:** steps of 0.95 / 0.85 / 1.3 s, the third about 0.4 s longer, only the last words of the line changing (Dds0y9pA33s); bedroom one, two, three at 1.1-1.3 s each in the tour.
- **List memes** (4 reels): the items change every 1.0-1.5 s over one held shot, speeding up (1.5, 1.5, 1.0, 1.0); the twist item comes last and stays to the end.
- **Endings:** inside the payoff, the reel just stops. No button, call to action or end card in 24 of 25.

### Transitions
- **Dips to black of 0.2-0.6 s around borrowed footage:** 20 of the 27 own cuts in ranks 13-25 are 0.2-0.3 s dips (DeNezexJYVA: all 6), and in ranks 1-12 dips appear in exactly the 6 reels that use borrowed clips: into and out of the clip, between slow "ritual" steps (DeLu5PTAKhL ×5), and on a card grid of about 1.94 s (Dd5rqFrABfS ×11).
- **Hard cuts** between the account's own shots, the phase montage, day/night pairs and picture-flip twists.
- **A black beat of 0.25 s or less** with the text swapped inside it resets each step of a rule of three (Dds0y9pA33s @ 1.0, 2.0); list memes put a 0.25 s dark pulse on each text change inside the held shot (4 of 13).
- One white flash into a hype insert (DdtMitiBCcc @ 4.80).

### Text
- **A premise line from 0.0 s in 25 of 25:** 3-12 words (median 5), small white type, no box, a faint shadow, lowercase or sentence case, in the style of Instagram's own text tool.
- **Position:** the top band (12-27% from the top) in 18 of 25; mid-frame (45-58%) on high-angle wides with empty floor in 5; lower (62-72%, under the face) in 2.
- **Lifetime:** to the end in about half; swapped exactly on the cut in the same place and style in a third ("Startup during the day" → "vs. night"); on the setup only in the rest.
- **Forms:** a quoted line said to the team (10 of 25), "pov:" (4), a template label ending in a colon ("Gang:", "What I actually do:"), a word cut off as the joke ("lim-"), at most one emoji.
- **Word captions only in the two tours.** No highlight words.

### Sound
- **The track replaces the location sound** in 21 of 25, at full level from frame 0: trending sounds credited to other accounts, licensed tracks ("Limitless", Naruto's "Pain's Theme", an "Empire State of Mind" instrumental), or the account's own posted sound. It is the soundtrack, not a bed.
- In ranks 1 and 9 the first action lands on the track's first hit (0.6-0.9 s).
- The narrated tour runs a bed about 4 dB under the voice; its cuts land 0.27 s after the last word and 0.33 s before the next (medians), 0.19 s between phrases inside a shot, B-roll of 1.0-1.5 s.
- The lip-sync's 0.97 s pause before "State?" is the joke: the engine's trim pass (pauses over 0.75 s) would cut it.

## Rules for the engine (meme skits)
1. **Length and shots:** 7-14 s (median about 10 s), 2-3 segments of own footage (up to 9 when the setup uses inserts).
2. **Audio:** `audio: mute` on every segment; the track as one `music` section from 0.0 to the end at `high`, started so a hit or the first vocal lands on the first action (0.6-0.9 s). No `captions`, no `sfx` unless the borrowed clip has its own.
3. **Text from frame 0:** one `on_screen_text` line, white, no box, small, lowercase or sentence case, 15-25% from the top over a wide (45-58% only over empty floor in a high-angle wide; 62-72% under the face on a close-up). Keep it to the end, or swap it on the payoff cut in the same place and style. Quotes for a line said to the cast, "pov:" for a situation.
4. **Setup:** one static segment, `zoom` {1.0, 1.0}, `beat: setup`, 2.5-5.9 s (target 3-4.3 s), at least 0.22 s per word of on-screen text. Never cut before 2.5 s.
5. **The twist cut** at 37-48% of the reel: on the frame the twist text appears when the new picture is the twist; otherwise 0.8-1.3 s after it, with the cast's reaction in the last 0.2-0.4 s before the cut. Put cut points on movements (an exit with the last people still in frame, the peak of a gesture, a phone lifting into its screen) or on sound events (a voice onset or a phrase end within one frame, the music returning, a lyric gap), not on a beat grid.
6. **Payoff:** 50-63% of the reel, labelled `punchline` or `reaction` so it is never trimmed: one held segment of 3-6 s, or a ping-pong of 0.7-1.0 s shots ending on a reaction held 1.3-1.5 s. End inside the payoff (or 0.4 s after the borrowed sound's phrase): no call to action, no end card.
7. **Transitions:** `cut` between own shots; `dip_to_black` 0.2-0.4 s into and out of borrowed footage, 0.4-0.6 s between slow ritual steps; a 0.1-0.25 s black beat (contiguous segments of one take with `dip_to_black` 0.2) on each step of a rule of three or each item of a list meme; `dip_to_white` 0.1-0.15 s into a hype insert.
8. **List memes:** the text changes every 1.0-1.5 s over one held shot, shortening toward the twist (1.5, 1.5, 1.0, 1.0); the twist item stays to the end.
9. **Phase montage:** 4-6 segments of 2.5-3.7 s, hard cuts on beats, a header for the whole reel at about 22% height and a numbered label per segment at about 70%.
10. **One-take formats** (lip-sync, tour, labelled characters): one segment, its pauses protected (put "pause" in the `purpose`).
11. **Narrated tour:** cuts 0.27 s after the last word and 0.33 s before the next, 0.19 s between phrases, `cutaway` B-roll of 1.0-1.5 s, a list in pictures at 1.1-1.3 s per item arriving 0.1-0.6 s before its noun, a bed about 4 dB under the voice, captions 2-5 words at 75-78%.

## Against the current skills
- **office-comedy-reels recipe C (meme post):** "1-4 shots" and "no captions" are confirmed. Wrong for this account: the caption line "at mid-frame (about 45%)" (18 of 25 use the top band), "for the whole video" (a third swap it on the cut), "music bed at medium" (the track is the whole soundtrack at full level), "the cut lands on the beat" (at chance outside montages).
- **office-comedy-reels recipe B (cast intro) with 2.2-2.6 s black-and-white freezes:** the "meet the team" reel (Dd5rqFrABfS) uses colour portraits of 1.4-1.8 s, a quoted claim and a roast line in a second colour, a dip every 2 s. Right for a fast meme cast intro; keep the 3D freeze for series openers.
- **clean-comedy-edit "hard cuts only", "subtitles only", "open on the strongest line in the first second", reactions of 0.6-1.2 s and punch-ins of 1.2-1.35:** all contradicted for meme posts (dips around borrowed clips, premise text in 25 of 25, a held silent setup, reactions of 1.5-5.4 s, no punch-ins). They may still fit dialogue skits; this account barely has any.
- **office-comedy-reels "never exceed 9 s without a cut or punch-in" and the punchline punch-in (1.0 → 1.3):** broken by 3 no-cut reels and never used.
- **The engine's trim pass and split edits** need guards: every meme setup is 2.7-5.9 s of silence by design, and automatic split edits must stay off in music-only reels and on sound-switch cuts.

## What the engine can't do yet
- Borrowed footage (12 of 25 pay off with one) and the letterboxed placement of a horizontal clip on black inside the vertical frame.
- The trending and licensed tracks the reels are cut to (the engine exports library music; the trend is added in the app), and finding a drop in them.
- The handheld tour move, word-by-word captions on screenshots with arrows, and the fire and flash overlays inside the borrowed clips.
- A dark pulse without a cut (approximated by splitting one take into contiguous segments with `dip_to_black` 0.2).

## Measurement notes
- The first detector pass missed nearly every dip to black (21 of 22 in ranks 1-12) and called them blurs; the corrected detector finds runs of near-black frames. Cut times on dips are 0.1-0.2 s off the real change.
- About 21 detected cuts in the top 12 live inside borrowed clips or a phone screen recording, which makes some reels look faster than the account's own editing (DeGDrsdgzv6 reads 0.30 s median shot; about 4.5 s by eye).
- Fixed text overlays made a few new shots read as "jump" cuts; most "freezes" are actors holding still or still images.
- Three transcripts are hallucinations over music, and the voice detector counts some sung lyrics as speech; treat speech numbers on music-only reels with caution.
