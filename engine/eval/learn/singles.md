## Creator talking heads (the "these kind of videos" set)

### Cuts
- **The voice is edited as one tight stream and the picture cuts between two words, not in pauses** (5 of 7): the voice detector finds 25-27 s blocks with no gap over 0.1 s (DeUUFJQu8cw 13.86-40.74, Dc_EqJhRPKx 0.10-25.63). The picture changes one word into a new sentence (DeUUFJQu8cw @ 15.20 "And | I know nothing") or about 0.1 s before the key word so the new shot arrives with it (Dehl5irvggc @ 0.87 "from | GTA?", DeUUFJQu8cw @ 40.37 "Tighter | cut"). When a cut does sit in a gap, the gap is 0.10-0.42 s (median 0.26 s over 17 gaps).
- **Pauses inside a shot:** medians 0.125-0.225 s, maximum about 0.36 s; the one exception is a deliberate 0.6-0.9 s beat while a proof card lands (Dehl5irvggc @ 6.7-7.3).
- **One cut per list item and per question/answer** (3 of 7): Dd6ue5FRZU2 cuts each part of its three-part hook (3 cuts in 2.3 s: @ 0.97, 1.77, 2.30) and splits every question from its answer; the answer gets the punch-in ("Fine", "Cool." held 0.56 s).
- **"Watch" is the hinge into the demo** in 3 of 7 (DeUUFJQu8cw @ 7.83, DePX9XMgDXx @ 14.00, Dehl5irvggc @ 4.90).
- **Endings:** all 4 talking reels end 0.0-0.36 s after the last word; 1 of 7 has an end card.

### Set-ups, sizes and transitions
- **Set-up changes every 4-8 s** for a sit-down talker (DeUUFJQu8cw 4.8-8.5 s), 5.7-7.1 s per speaker in the multi-speaker countdown (Dc_EqJhRPKx), 2.5-4.6 s for walk-and-talk (Dehl5irvggc).
- **Most set-up changes are whips:** 13 of 16 set-up, speaker or location changes in the three slower reels are 0.2-0.5 s whip or zoom blurs (DeUUFJQu8cw 6 of 8, Dc_EqJhRPKx 4 of 4, Dehl5irvggc 3 of 4). In Dc_EqJhRPKx the whip starts on the outgoing speaker's last word and the next voice follows within 0.0-0.15 s; each new speaker starts 0.53-0.91 s before her countdown number. Only the fastest cutter (Dd6ue5FRZU2, median shot 1.23 s) hard-cuts all 7 of its set-up changes.
- **Inside one set-up, the fastest reel cuts every 1.6 s** by punching in and out: 11 punches at a steady x1.22 (out x0.81-0.82) on a full-body wide, which reads fine although it is under 0.3. Same-size jumps appear in 3 of 7, hidden by a card, a hero word or a 1-2 frame white flash on the same frame (Dd6ue5FRZU2 @ 0.97, Dc_EqJhRPKx @ 2.60). 4 of 7 use no digital zoom at all: their variety comes from the set-ups (W / M / MW / W / M).
- A 1-2 frame white flash on the hook's first cut in 2-3 of 7.

### Inserts, text and sound
- **Cards over the speaker, on the keyword within 0.1-0.3 s** (4 of 7), far more often than full-screen cutaways: DeUUFJQu8cw illustrates about 24 phrases in 54 s (one every 2.2 s). No cutaway B-roll footage in any of the 7.
- **Captions:** word-timed on 83-99% of frames, 1-3 words per screen, one accent word per line in italic serif or gold, 17-20 text changes per 10 s.
- **Hero words** appear on the spoken onset (within 0.1 s) and hold 0.5-1.5 s (median about 1 s; countdown numbers 0.5-1.0 s; a section closer up to 2.5 s); 2 of 7 put one behind the head.
- **A grade change on the spoken word** ("go dark", DeUUFJQu8cw @ 24.43), not after the line.
- **Sound:** 3 of 7 are voice only; Dehl5irvggc has a bed 3.8 dB under the voice. Cuts are on the beat no more than chance.
- **Hook:** text at frame 0 in 6 of 7, first word within 0.25 s in the talking reels, first cut by 0.23-2.6 s. A comment-keyword CTA in 4 of 7, but only 1 makes the keyword the biggest word.

## Rules for the engine
1. **Creator set-up changes:** a new `clip` (location, speaker) every 4-8 s (2.5-4.5 s walk-and-talk, 5.5-7 s per speaker), each with `transition_in: slide_left` 0.3 s, or `zoom_in` 0.3 s when the new shot is much wider or closer; `cut` inside a set-up. When the reel cuts every 1-2 s, hard-cut the set-up changes too.
2. **Where the set-up change goes:** the next segment's `say` starts on the first or second word of a new sentence, or on the word that names what the new shot shows. Never inside a word, except under a whip, where the outgoing speaker's last word may run under the blur.
3. **Inside a set-up:** one segment per 1.2-2.5 s, split at list items and between a question and its answer; alternate `zoom` 1.0 and 1.22 on a wide, 1.0 and 1.3+ on a chest-up source; the answer and a one-word aside get the tighter size. A same-size jump only with a `card`, a hero title or a 0.05 s `dip_to_white` starting on the same frame.
4. **Pauses:** creator and realtor reels keep 0.12-0.25 s between phrases and nothing over 0.3 s inside a shot (one 0.6-0.9 s beat allowed while a proof card lands). The trim pass's 0.75 s threshold is far too loose for them.
5. **Multi-speaker:** no gap at a speaker change (0.0-0.15 s) under a whip; open each speaker's segment 0.5-0.9 s before her key word or number so it can get a hero title.
6. **Hero titles** on the word's onset, held 0.8-1.5 s (numbers 0.5-1.0 s), about one every 2 s, 2-4 per reel `behind_subject`; captions 1-3 words at 55-75% of the height with one accent word per line.
7. **Mood or grade changes on the word that names them:** split the segment there and give the second part the new `adjust`.
8. **Realtor style:** cuts only at sentence ends (a 0.12 s tail on cuts between set-ups), a new room every 5-7 s with a size change at each, one emphasis zoom per 10-15 s (three contiguous segments: 1.0 → 1.6 in 0.3-0.5 s, hold 1-2 s, 1.6 → 1.0 in 0.5-0.7 s), `dip_to_white` 0.15-0.2 s on up to 3 argument turns, no B-roll: graphics on the spoken word.
9. **Endings:** the last segment ends on the engine's 0.2 s out point; the keyword title stays to the last frame; no end card.
10. **Friend-types comedy:** one `hold` of 2.0-2.4 s per type (3.0 s for the first and the closing group), starting 0.0-0.4 s after that friend's defining line; live segments of 3-8 s between holds; a song under the whole reel near voice level so it fills every hold.

## Against the current skills
- **creator-reels:** "at most 2 whips" (the slower creators whip on most set-up changes), "hero words hold 1.5-2.5 s" (0.5-1.5 s), "end within 0.5-1 s of the last word" (0.0-0.36 s), "grade after the line" (on the word), "dip_to_white only on the hook" (realtors use up to 3 on argument turns), "2-3 words per caption" (realtors use 1). The reels are right for these styles.
- **punch-ins-and-reframing "change the size by at least 0.3":** 1.22 on a full-body wide reads fine, and same-size jumps work under a graphic; keep 0.3 for chest-up sources without graphics.
- **talking-head "emphasis punch-ins 1.08-1.15, rare" and "a clean frame with no text on the pivot lines":** the realtors use ramped 1.5-1.7x zooms every 15 s and put their biggest title and a flash on the pivots. Right for social reels; the small static punch-in stays right for hiding a jump cut.
- **The trim pass** (pauses over 0.75 s) is far too loose for creator and realtor reels (about 0.3 s), and the automatic J-cut should not apply under a whip.
- **reel-dbocjkssdqt, reel-ddo63lgpbsn, reel-dxzurbatrqt** each had measured claims that the frames contradict (cut counts, shot lengths, act times, tempo, transitions, music); corrected drafts are in skills-draft.
- **office-comedy-reels for DPzmN55D8BK:** the freeze length (2.2-2.6 s) is confirmed; but here the freeze greys the background and keeps the person in colour, a song runs under all the dialogue, and the POV line stays about 22 s.

## What the engine can't do yet
- A motion-blurred whip (13 set-up changes need one); `slide_left` and `zoom_in` are clean moves.
- Subject cut-out effects: the speaker on a solid colour with type behind him, and a freeze with a grey background and the person in colour.
- Animated UI mock-ups (typing prompt bars, timelines, DM and comment bubbles, stats cards, a before/after wipe line), a wall of reels framing the speaker, a picture-in-picture of the reel itself, title effects (sparks, rings, maps), and fully animated ads like DePX9XMgDXx (16:9 landscape).
- Motion-graphics morphs and drawn paths (DXzurbATRQT), the 3D phone fly-through and letters on arcs (DdO63LGPBsn): these need bespoke compositions, not templates.

## Measurement notes
- Of 96 detected cuts in the creator set, 12 were not edits (graphic or grade changes, card exits), 19 were edits but not shot changes (17 freeze edges, 2 UI slides) and about 7 were missed (animated scene changes). In the other six, 38 detected, about 32 real.
- The first detector pass caught none of the realtors' white flashes and called most of them blurs; the corrected detector labels a brightness spike inside a transition as a flash.
- Freeze starts were reported about 0.65 s late in DPzmN55D8BK (a title animates in over the freeze); the corrected analysis starts a freeze on the edit that stops the picture.
- A loud song hid the voices from the voice detector in DPzmN55D8BK, and whisper transcribed the lyrics as dialogue.
